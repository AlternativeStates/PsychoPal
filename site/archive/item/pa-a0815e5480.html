BEYOND CROSS-SECTIONAL ANALYSIS

Alessio Giacomel
Research Portal (King's College London) · 2026-11-01
PETHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformComputational / whole-brain modellingClinical trial / evidence synthesisMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)

Background The use of molecular neuroimaging as a quantitative biomarker for clinical applications, however, remains limited.

Methods One limiting factor is the prevalent use of cross-sectional frameworks for analysing neuroimaging data, rather than single-subject statistics.

Results Additionally, I illustrate how these harmonized data can be integrated and utilized to establish a normative model for healthy populations, which can explain significant portions of the variability within them. For the model estimation in this part, I utilize historical data to underscore the importance of existing normative data in supporting future clinical trials.
