Testing an accelerated transcranial magnetic stimulation protocol for methamphetamine use disorder — study protocol

Addiction Science & Clinical Practice · 2026-11-13 · doi:10.1186/s13722-026-00742-7
TMS / TMS-EEGSubstance use (other)ClinicalPeer reviewedBrain stimulationClinical trial / evidence synthesis

Aim This study aims to evaluate the safety, feasibility, and preliminary efficacy of an accelerated intermittent theta-burst stimulation (iTBS) protocol applied to the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) for individuals with moderate …

Methods This single-site, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled (1:1 active: sham) pilot trial seeks to randomize 20 adults with moderate to severe MUD.

Results If this pilot protocol proves safe and feasible, and provides preliminary evidence of efficacy, it would prompt future well-powered studies.
