Therapeutic Harmony: Combining Music Therapy and Psychedelic Treatments in Psychiatric Care

Dominikus David Biondi Situmorang, Nadiya Kurniati, Gita Arisara, Listya Istiningtyas, Rose Mini Agoes Salim
Buletin Konseling Inovatif · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.17977/2797-9954.1181
Psychedelics (general)Depression (incl. TRD/MDD)PTSDAnxietyClinicalPeer reviewedInformation theory / complexity#music therapy#psychedelics#psychiatry

Aim Whether combining music therapy with psychedelic treatment can improve recovery and symptoms in psychiatric disorders.

Methods Integrative narrative review of studies addressing music therapy, psychedelic treatment, and their combination in psychiatric care.

Results Both treatments were found to reduce symptoms of anxiety, depression and PTSD and to speed patient recovery. Long-term effects and the mechanisms of their interaction remain under-researched.

Conclusion The combination has great potential to improve psychiatric care, though research gaps, particularly long-term effects and mechanisms, must be filled.

Why it matters for you: Music-assisted psychedelic therapy is a setting factor relevant to your psychedelic clinical pharmacology and mechanistic interests.
