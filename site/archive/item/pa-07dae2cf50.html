Mechanism-aligned ketamine therapy for treatment-resistant depression: a 2026 update—toward an integrative mechanistic framework for clinical translation

Sherry-Anne Muscat, Glenn Hartelius
Frontiers in Psychiatry · 2026-11-20 · doi:10.3389/fpsyt.2026.1966279
Ketamine / esketamineDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)PTSDClinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modelling#ketamine#treatment-resistant depression#entropic brain

Aim Propose an integrative mechanistic framework for ketamine therapy in treatment-resistant depression, updating a 2021 systems-level model with literature through April 2026.

Methods Narrative review integrating evidence on NMDAR modulation, neuroplastogenesis, anti-inflammatory signalling, entropic brain states and psychedelic phenomenology, with attention to chronic stress and attachment as cross-cutting factors.

Results The review synthesises five interacting domains proposed to underlie rapid ketamine effects and considers how developmental and relational factors may shape treatment resistance across depression, PTSD and suicidality.

Conclusion The framework aims to generate testable predictions about patient selection, timing and durability of ketamine response, pending controlled evaluation.

Why it matters for you: Covers ketamine mechanisms with an entropic brain state component, relevant to your ketamine neuroplastogen and neurodynamics research.
