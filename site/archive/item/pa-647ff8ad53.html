SV2A PET imaging in Parkinson's disease and atypical parkinsonism: A systematic review of neuroimaging methodologies and regional findings.

Chiara Camastra, Carme Uribe, Angenelle Eve Rosal, Caitilin Chow, Mary Elizabeth Coulombe, Alex Murgoci, Andrea Quattrone, Antonio P Strafella
Journal of Parkinson's disease · 2026-11-07 · doi:10.1177/1877718x261505655
PETParkinson'sClinicalPeer reviewedClinical trial / evidence synthesisMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#sv2a pet#synaptic density#parkinsonism

Aim To review neuroimaging methods and regional findings from SV2A PET in Parkinson's disease and atypical parkinsonian disorders.

Methods Systematic review following PRISMA 2020 of PubMed, Scopus and Web of Science; 14 studies using validated SV2A PET radiotracers in parkinsonian populations; risk of bias with QUADAS-2; narrative synthesis as heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis.

Results In PD the substantia nigra was most affected, with striatal involvement in advanced disease. In PSP and CBD synaptic loss was widespread and exceeded grey matter atrophy, falling about 3-4% per year in some longitudinal regions. In MSA infratentorial involvement predominated, with AUC up to 0.99.

Conclusion The authors conclude SV2A PET shows disease-specific presynaptic patterns supporting its use as a diagnostic and monitoring biomarker. They call for methodological harmonisation and longitudinal multicentre studies.
