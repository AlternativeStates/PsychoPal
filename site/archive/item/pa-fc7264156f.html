Rapid antidepressant effects of psilocybin without increased SV2A binding: baseline binding predicts response

H. Yngwe, P. Plaven Sigray, M. Andersson, M. Bolin, M. Beckman, C.-J. Ekman, M. Tiger, J. Lundberg
medrxiv (psychiatry and clinical psychology) · 2026-11-08 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.06.26367247
PsilocybinPETDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalPreprintComputational / whole-brain modellingClinical trial / evidence synthesisMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)

We report a pre-registered [11C]UCB-J PET sub-study of a recently published randomized trial in which a single psilocybin dose produced rapid and sustained antidepressant effects in major depressive disorder (MDD).
