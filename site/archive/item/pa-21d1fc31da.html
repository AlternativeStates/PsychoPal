Revisiting entropy and entropy-related magnitudes of a symbolic sequence and their estimation.

E Aragón-Pérez, A Mesa-Rodríguez, K García-Medina, D Estevez-Moya, A Mollineda-Rodríguez, E Estevez-Rams
Chaos (Woodbury, N.Y.) · 2026 · doi:10.1063/5.0359940
Peer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformConnectivity / synchronyInformation theory / complexityBrain–body physiologyMachine learning / AIClinical trial / evidence synthesis#entropy#lempel-ziv#eeg complexity

Aim To revisit entropy, entropy density, effective measure complexity and informational distance, and how to estimate them for sequential data.

Methods Theoretical review linking Shannon entropy and Kolmogorov complexity, with estimation via Lempel-Ziv factorisation and an open-source parallel software library; three case studies on fMRI, coupled oscillators and ECG.

Results Ziv's theorem gives asymptotic equivalence between normalised distinct-word counts and entropy density, supporting estimation of these measures for finite sequences. Case studies illustrate applications to brain connectivity, oscillator dynamics and atrial fibrillation classification.

Conclusion The framework captures structural and dynamical properties across domains, with practical estimation tools for finite data.

Why it matters for you: Strongly tied to your EEG complexity and information-theoretic neurodynamics work, and offers a ready estimation library for Lempel-Ziv measures.
