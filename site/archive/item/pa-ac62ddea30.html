Conservation of the tumour microenvironment across organs and species.

Michael Januszyk, John M Lu, Maria Korah, Serena L Jing, Peter Y Xie, Mahsa Fallah, Benjamin Pham, Jason L Guo, Charlotte E Berry, Helen H Wang, Heather E Talbott, Kevin Nee, Thalia Le, Cesar A Garcia, Amanda Gonçalves, Fatemeh Nosrati, Khristian E Bauer-Rowe, Chuner Guo, James P Agolia, Rosyli Y Reveron-Thornton, Norah E Liang, Antonio Tomasso, Lionel E Kameni, Sarah E DiIorio, Jennifer B L Parker, Annah G Morgan, Michelle Griffin, Teri A Longacre, Deshka S Foster, Derrick C Wan, Jeffrey A Norton, Daniel Delitto, Michael T Longaker
Nature · 2026-11-18 · doi:10.1038/s41586-026-11270-4
Pre-clinicalPeer reviewed

Aim Here we conduct a comprehensive pan-cancer analysis by algorithmically screening thousands of publicly available single-cell transcriptomic datasets for stromal and immune signatures across species and tissue types.

Results We demonstrate conserved hallmarks of host cell transcriptional programming in response to malignancy across 42 cancer types in more than 2,000 human and mouse tumour samples.

Conclusion These findings are codified as an interactive web atlas ( tmeatlas.stanford.edu ) and software package (scTME) that will serve as practical resources for the systematic interrogation of the TME in tumours.
