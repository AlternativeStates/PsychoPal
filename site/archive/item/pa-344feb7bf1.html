Psilocybin modulates nucleus accumbens activity via astrocytic transcriptional regulation.

Su Min Kang, Dae-Si Kang, Tae-Yong Choi, Sejin Jeong, A-Hyeon Lee, Yujeong Jeong, Minjin Oh, Eum Ji Kim, Jieun Lee, Ki Jung Kim, C Justin Lee, Na Young Lim, Tae Woong Na, Bong Hyo Lee, Jae-Sung Bae, Su-Jeong Park, Suchan Chang, Ja Wook Koo
Experimental & molecular medicine · 2026-11-05 · doi:10.1038/s12276-026-01872-3
PsilocybinPre-clinicalPeer reviewedPre-clinical / invasive#psilocybin#astrocytes#gaba

Aim How psilocybin and related tryptamines modulate inhibitory tone in the nucleus accumbens, and whether astrocytes contribute.

Methods Behavioural drug discrimination, in vivo neurochemistry, ex vivo whole-cell recordings, cell-type-specific GABA measures, ChIP-qPCR and calcium imaging in rodent nucleus accumbens tissue.

Results Psilocybin-like tryptamines increased NAc neuronal activity and reduced extracellular GABA. Psilocin reduced tonic GABA current without affecting miniature IPSCs; psilocybin lowered astrocytic GABA, raised NFATc4 and ALDH5A1, and its calcium responses were blocked by 5-HT2A antagonism.

Conclusion The authors propose psilocybin modulates mesolimbic inhibitory tone through astrocyte-associated transcriptional regulation of GABA homeostasis, a mechanism linked to 5-HT2A signalling.

Why it matters for you: Adds an astrocytic, GABA-based mechanism to psilocybin action, relevant to your interest in neurodynamics and excitation-inhibition balance.
