18F-Labeled 7-Azaindole-Based Radioligands for Imaging Colony-Stimulating Factor 1 Receptor in Glioblastoma

Xiaodan An, Yuqing Ning, Wei Wei, Linjiang Tong, Yaxin Cheng, Tao Yang, Jin Du, Xiaojun Zhang, Hua Xie, Yiyun Henry Huang, Hongmei Jia
Journal of Medicinal Chemistry · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.1021/acs.jmedchem.6c01759
PETPre-clinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingPre-clinical / invasiveMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)

Background A series of 7-azaindole-based compounds were designed and synthesized as novel colony-stimulating factor 1 receptor (CSF1R) ligands.

Methods Among them, [18F]PLX6 (IC50 = 12.3 nM) showed modest brain uptake and rapid clearance in rats. Micro-PET/CT imaging resulted in clear visualization of both subcutaneous and intracranial orthotopic U87MG gliomas.

Results Treatment with PLX3397 or PLX5622 significantly reduced tumor uptake, indicating specific binding of [18F]PLX6 to CSF1R. Ex vivo autoradiography at 30 min postinjection (p.i.) confirmed high tumor-to-brain ratio (10.14 ± 1.52) and specific binding of [18F]PLX6 to CSF1R.

Conclusion These results support further evaluation of [18F]PLX6 as a promising PET imaging agent for noninvasive visualization of CSF1R expression in glioblastoma.
