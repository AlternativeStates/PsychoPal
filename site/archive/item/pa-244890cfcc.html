High-Quality Genome Assembly and Transcriptomic Analysis Reveal Potential Pathways for Psilocybin Biosynthesis in Gymnopilus dilepis.

Sen Yao, Yuanyuan Liu, Weiwei Xia, Yue Xia, Kai Yang, Hui Li, Baogui Xie
Journal of fungi (Basel, Switzerland) · 2026-10-28 · doi:10.3390/jof12110811
PsilocybinDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)AnxietyPeer reviewed#psilocybin biosynthesis#fungi#genomics

Aim To assemble a high-quality genome for Gymnopilus dilepis and identify pathways that may supply precursors for psilocybin biosynthesis.

Methods Chromosome-level genome assembly, evolutionary analysis against related fungi, and transcriptomic analysis of psilocybin-associated metabolic pathways.

Results The genome has 13 chromosomes, spans 54.2 Mb and contains 15,211 predicted genes. Psilocybin biosynthesis was linked to cysteine, methionine, arginine, proline and tryptophan metabolism.

Conclusion The assembly and amino acid metabolism link provide a basis for exploring the genes regulating psilocybin biosynthesis in this species.

Why it matters for you: Relevant to your interest in the biosynthesis and chemistry of tryptamines, though only loosely connected to your own work.
