Adapted Massed Exposure Therapy Enhanced with MDMA for PTSD (aMETEMP): A randomized, double-masked protocol adapted for Veterans Affairs healthcare system.

L A Morland, B Chargin, B Rothbaum, J L Maples-Keller, T Wachsman, S B Norman, S N Adams, D Perivoliotis, A Alam, C S Stauffer, A C Angkaw, V Marquez, J Woolley, J M Kopelman, P P Schnurr
Contemporary clinical trials communications · 2026-10-27 · doi:10.1016/j.conctc.2026.101705
Substance use (other)PTSDClinicalPeer reviewedClinical trial / evidence synthesis#mdma#ptsd#trial design

Aim To describe the protocol of a pilot trial testing massed prolonged exposure therapy enhanced with MDMA for PTSD in US veterans.

Methods Randomised, double-masked pilot protocol with up to 20 veterans, MDMA dosing of 35 to 125 mg within 28-30 hours of therapy over 11 visits, with an incomplete disclosure masking strategy.

Conclusion The authors aim to provide preliminary feasibility, safety, masking integrity and symptom-change signals for this adapted MDMA-assisted protocol in a VA outpatient setting.

Why it matters for you: Useful for your psychedelic clinical work as a methodological example of masking and incomplete disclosure in MDMA-assisted therapy trials.
