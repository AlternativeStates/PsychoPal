Characterising cortical perturbation-recovery dynamics using TMS-EEG: The Brain Dynamic Toolbox

Wei Qin, Andre D. H. Peterson, Anthony Neville Burkitt, Paul Bernard Fitzgerald, Phillippa Karoly, Jodie Naim‐Feil
bioRxiv (Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory) · 2026-11-04 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.30.763068
EEGTMS / TMS-EEGHealthy volunteersClinicalPreprintComputational / whole-brain modellingBrain stimulationPreprocessing / source / tools

Aim To adapt dynamical measures used in computational and experimental perturbation models to human transcranial magnetic stimulation- electroencephalography (TMS-EEG) and determine whether analysis of the extended post-stimulation trajectory reveals additional features …

Methods Single-pulse and paired-pulse LICI TMS-EEG responses from the bilateral dorsolateral prefrontal cortex were re-analysed in 16 healthy adults.

Results PILI distinguished conditions in the earliest post-stimulation window before converging, whereas ACFW differences persisted across a broader early interval. LE showed weaker and less consistent discrimination.

Conclusion Together, these measures provide complementary characterisation of the evolving response to inhibitory conditioning and are implemented in the user- friendly, open-source Brain Dynamic Metric Toolbox to support reproducible analysis and further physiological and clinical …
