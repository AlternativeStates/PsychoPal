Chronic Mild Stress in Male Wistar-Kyoto Rats Induces Long-Term Hippocampal Plasticity Impairments Differentially Reversed by Acute and Chronic Ketamine Treatment.

Vincent Loizeau, Mila Jesic, Robin Kuster, Sylvain Hugel, Alexandra Barbelivien, Lucas Lecourtier
The European journal of neuroscience · 2026 · doi:10.1111/ejn.70755
Ketamine / esketamineDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)Pre-clinicalPeer reviewed

Aim Here, we investigated how chronic mild stress and subsequent acute and chronic ketamine treatments modulate synaptic plasticity in the Schaffer collateral pathway in the dorsal hippocampus of stress-vulnerable Wistar-Kyoto rats.

Methods Adult male rats were subjected to 4 weeks of chronic mild stress and vulnerability was determined through behavioral performance in the forced swim and elevated plus maze tests.

Results Acute ketamine restored normal LTP but not LTD, while chronic ketamine restored LTD but not LTP.

Conclusion These findings highlight complex temporal dynamics in the modulation of hippocampal synaptic plasticity by ketamine following chronic stress and demonstrate that acute and chronic treatment regimens differentially affect the induction of LTP and LTD.
