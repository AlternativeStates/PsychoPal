Spectral and complexity-based signatures of advanced concentration absorption meditation: A high-density EEG study of jhana

Peter Moltke Skov-Andersen, Remko van Lutterveld, Sofie Paludanus, Thomas Cahaly, Ruby M. Potash, Andrés Canales‐Johnson, Winson Fu Zun Yang, Matthew D. Sacchet
bioRxiv (Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory) · 2026-11-18 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.15.766880
PreprintSpectral / aperiodic / waveformInformation theory / complexityComputational / whole-brain modelling#meditation#eeg complexity#aperiodic activity

Aim To characterise spectral and entropy-based EEG signatures of each jhana state of advanced concentration absorption meditation and relate them to experience.

Methods High-density EEG in 20 advanced meditators across eight ACAM-J states, with a multidimensional battery of spectral and complexity measures and phenomenological ratings.

Results Widespread posterior-central reductions in oscillatory and aperiodic power, posterior aperiodic flattening, and rising entropy-based complexity. Complexity stayed elevated in the most minimal states, and between practitioners higher entropy accompanied visual and auditory content.

Conclusion ACAM-J is a non-pharmacological model state at the high-complexity end of altered consciousness, revealing a dissociation between neural complexity and experiential richness.

Why it matters for you: Directly connects to your EEG complexity, aperiodic activity and interoception interests, using a meditation model of altered consciousness.
