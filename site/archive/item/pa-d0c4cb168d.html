Long-term dysregulation of histone acetylation, BDNF expression, and the hippocampal transcriptome in adult rats exhibiting a depressive-like phenotype following repeated chlorpyrifos exposure

Ana C.R. Ribeiro, Elisa Hawkins, Fay M. Jahr, Mikhail Dozmorov, Joseph L. McClay, Laxmikant S. Deshpande
bioRxiv (Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory) · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.02.763669
Depression (incl. TRD/MDD)Pre-clinicalPreprintComputational / whole-brain modelling

Aim In this study, we use a rat model of occupational-like CPF exposure associated with a chronic depressive phenotype to investigate underlying molecular mechanisms.

Methods Adult rats received CPF (5 mg/kg/day) for 21 days. We used chromatin immunoprecipitation to assay histone 3 lysine 9 acetylation (H3K9ac) occupancy at the Bdnf promoter IV.

Results H3K9ac levels at the Bdnf promoter IV were significantly reduced in CPF rats, accompanied by a concomitant reduction in BDNF protein expression.

Conclusion Overall, this study indicates persistent epigenetic and transcriptional dysregulation associated with reduced hippocampal BDNF and alterations in neuronal plasticity-related pathways linked to a long-term depressive-like phenotype following CPF exposure.
