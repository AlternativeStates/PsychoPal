The C. elegans Connectome Toolbox: an open, extensible framework for multimodal connectomic data integration and analysis

Y. Vickneswaran, A. Ponzi, A. Sinha, N. Cohen, S. Larson, P. Gleeson
biorxiv (neuroscience) · 2026-11-05 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.04.764366
PreprintConnectivity / synchronyComputational / whole-brain modellingPreprocessing / source / tools

The nematode C. elegans is one of the best studied model organisms in biology. Since the initial description of the C. elegans neuronal connectome by White and colleagues, numerous studies have mapped and analyzed the connectome, and released structured datasets of the connectivity, often merging and adding to previous versions.
