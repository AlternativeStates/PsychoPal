Physics-informed neural networks for subject-specific recovery of travelling-wave structure in resting-state fMRI

Pouya Taghipour L., Roberto C. Sotero
BIO Web of Conferences · 2026-11-18 · doi:10.1051/bioconf/202626701002
fMRIClinicalPeer reviewedConnectivity / synchronyComputational / whole-brain modellingTravelling wavesMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#travelling waves#physics-informed neural networks#resting-state fmri

Aim Recover subject-specific travelling-wave structure from resting-state fMRI using a physics-informed neural network with a structural connectivity prior.

Methods A wave-informed physics-informed neural network modelling BOLD as seven damped travelling-wave generators anchored to Schaefer networks, validated on synthetic data and applied to 41 cognitively normal ADNI-3 participants with a diffusion-tensor prior.

Results The structural prior modestly but significantly increased alignment between recovered wave directions and white-matter geometry (one-sided Wilcoxon p = 0.026), with exploratory effects in visual, dorsal-attention and salience networks.

Conclusion The network-anchored parametric method returns subject-specific propagation descriptors from short scans, with the structural prior giving a small detectable benefit.

Why it matters for you: Subject-specific travelling-wave recovery from resting-state fMRI addresses your interests in travelling waves and whole-brain models.
