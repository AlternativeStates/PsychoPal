Quantitative Analysis of Lysergic Acid Diethylamide: A Ring Study Among Five Swiss Drug Checking Services.

Manuela Carla Monti, Emilia Gianola, Raoul P J Koning, Mireia Ventura, Pieter E Oomen
Drug testing and analysis · 2026-11-14 · doi:10.1002/dta.70190
LSDPeer reviewedInformation theory / complexityClinical trial / evidence synthesis

Aim This study presents an interlaboratory ring trial on LSD quantification within the context of drug checking.

Methods Five Swiss DCS laboratories participated, each employing different analytical techniques (HPLC-DAD, GC-MS, and HPLC-MS/MS) and extraction procedures. Three sample types were distributed: two LSD-infused blotters and one methanolic LSD solution.

Results Interlaboratory variability was low, with relative standard deviations of 8%-16.5% and mean accuracy ranging from -5.3% to -13.0%. Variability was higher for the liquid sample.

Conclusion Reliable chemical data is essential for accurate dosing and effective harm reduction.
