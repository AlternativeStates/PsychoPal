Autonomic Reactivity and EEG-defined Clusters Reveal Distinct Chronic Pain Profiles.

Pin-Yang Yeh, Chih-Lung Lin
The Clinical journal of pain · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.1097/ajp.0000000000001424
EEGDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalPeer reviewedBrain–body physiologyPreprocessing / source / toolsPre-clinical / invasive

Aim This study examined whether integrating stress-related heart rate variability (HRV) with resting-state electroencephalography (EEG) could characterize physiological subgroups in Chronic pain after spine surgery (CPSS).

Methods A total of 177 patients completed pain- and emotion-related questionnaires, a 3-minute resting-state EEG recording, and HRV assessments during baseline, mental arithmetic stress, and recovery.

Results LF/HF -decreased group (n = 56) reported higher depressive symptom scores than the increase group (n = 121, P = 0.045), without significant pain differences.

Conclusion These exploratory findings, without study-wide multiplicity adjustment, require independent validation before clinical application.
