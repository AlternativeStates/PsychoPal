Clinical Outcomes and Corticomotor Excitability After Accelerated High-Frequency rTMS in Refractory Trigeminal Neuralgia: A Prospective Comparative Cohort Study

Polya Ivanova Lambeva, Plamen Stoyanov Bozhinov, Pencho Tonchev, Stoyan Plamenov Bozhinov
Brain Sciences · 2026-11-04 · doi:10.3390/brainsci16111208
TMS / TMS-EEGClinicalPeer reviewedERP / MMN / predictive codingBrain stimulationMachine learning / AI

Aim The objective of this study is to evaluate three-month changes in corticomotor excitability, assessed by motor-evoked potential (MEP) amplitudes, following accelerated high-frequency repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) of the primary motor cortex …

Methods This prospective, non-randomized, parallel-group comparative cohort study enrolled 192 patients with refractory classical TN, diagnosed according to the International Classification of Headache Disorders, third edition (ICHD-3).

Results At 3 months, mean facial MEP amplitudes decreased within the active rTMS group (58.11 ± 12.77 μV to 49.77 ± 12.49 μV; p < 0.001), indicating a temporal association with reduced local cortical excitability.

Conclusion These findings may indicate preserved cortical neuroplastic capacity in older, highly treatment-resistant populations, potentially supporting accelerated M1 rTMS as an age-resilient therapeutic strategy for chronic neuropathic pain, although a direct causal relationship remains …
