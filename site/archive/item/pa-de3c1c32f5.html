An Integrated Chronic Cisterna Magna Access Preparation for Glymphatic MRI in Awake and Mechanically Ventilated Anesthetized Mice.

Juchen Li, Tianming Zha, Binshi Bo, Kaiwei Zhang, Chuanjun Tong, Ming Jiang, Xinye Qian, Yufeng Li, Jing Cang, Fang Fang
Journal of visualized experiments : JoVE · 2026-11-13 · doi:10.3791/74496
Ketamine / esketaminePre-clinicalPeer reviewedBrain–body physiology#glymphatic mri#dce-mri#csf tracer

Aim To describe a standardised mouse preparation enabling intracisternal tracer delivery and dynamic contrast-enhanced MRI across awake and anaesthetised states.

Methods Chronic cisterna magna catheter mechanically linked to an implanted head holder; graded habituation for awake imaging; intubation and mechanical ventilation for anaesthetised imaging; serial 3D T1-weighted imaging during intracisternal Gd-DTPA infusion.

Results Representative data show stable intracisternal access, manageable motion in awake acquisition, and interpretable spatiotemporal signal changes under awake, isoflurane and ketamine/xylazine conditions. No quantitative comparison is reported.

Conclusion The authors suggest the framework will support comparative and longitudinal studies of CSF tracer transport across physiological and anaesthetic conditions. It is a methods protocol with representative rather than statistical data.

Why it matters for you: Relevant to your MRI and PET methods work and to the glymphatic and CSF-transport questions that sit alongside neurodynamics.
