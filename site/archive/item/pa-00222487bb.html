A Comparison and Validation of BIDS Apps for Preprocessing of [ 11 C]UCB-J PET data

Guusje Haver, Granville James Matheson, Hampus Yngwe, Max Andersson, Niklas K. Edvall, Martin Noergaard, Francesca Zanderigo, Mike Schmidt, Elizabeth A. Bartlett, Jeffrey Morris Miller, Zsolt Cselényi, Mikael Tiger, Maria Beckman, Carl Johan Ekman, Johan Lundberg
bioRxiv (Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory) · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.28.762181
PETClinicalPreprintPreprocessing / source / toolsClinical trial / evidence synthesisMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)

Aim In this study, two of these apps were validated against in-house software.

Methods Data were acquired in-house from 14 subjects who received placebo in a randomized controlled clinical trial and underwent two PET measurements using the radioligand [ 11 C]UCB-J. Preprocessing was performed with two BIDS Apps …

Results V T values for all 9 regions of interest were highly consistent across tools with estimates of Intraclass Correlation Coefficients for consistency ranging between 0.94 and >0.99, although bias between tools measured as signed Percentage Difference was present, ranging from 0.1% - 10.2% depending on the region.

Conclusion Region-dependant bias between tools warrants caution for direct comparison of V T between tools.
