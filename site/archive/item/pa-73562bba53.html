Vasomechanic treatment is associated with increased efflux of human brain proteins.

Johanna Tuunanen, Katariina Hautamäki, Tommi Väyrynen, Matti Järvelä, Vesa Korhonen, Niko Huotari, Mika Kaakinen, Janne Kananen, Heta Helakari, Lauri Raitamaa, Jari Jukkola, Sanna-Kaisa Herukka, Katariina Laurén, Ulla Salmi, Lauri Eklund, Juha Nikkinen, Maiken Nedergaard, Vesa Kiviniemi
Nature communications · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.1038/s41467-026-78894-y
Dementia / Alzheimer'sPeer reviewed

An age-related neurovascular impairment in nitric oxide (NO)-mediated vasodilation and reciprocal Piezo1-dependent vascular vasoconstriction may precede the accumulation of soluble brain proteins such as amyloid-β (Aβ) and disruption of brain fluid homeostasis in Alzheimer's disease (AD). Preclinical studies suggest that restoring these pathways can enhance neurofluid transport and improve cognitive outcomes.
