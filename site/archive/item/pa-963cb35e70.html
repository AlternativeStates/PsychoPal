Rapid-Acting Antidepressants: Targeting Neuroplasticity Beyond Monoamines.

Saumya Das, Avijit Mazumder, Deepanshi Rana
Central nervous system agents in medicinal chemistry · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.2174/0118715249485751261025102238
Ketamine / esketamineDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)Peer reviewedPre-clinical / invasive#rapid antidepressants#neuroplasticity#ketamine

Aim To review rapid-acting antidepressants that target neuroplasticity beyond monoamine pathways.

Methods Narrative review of ketamine, psychedelics, neurosteroids and investigational NMDA modulators, focusing on synaptic scaling, dendritic spine formation and translational issues.

Conclusion Ketamine acts rapidly through glutamate release, AMPA receptor activation, BDNF-TrkB and mTOR signalling, but translational, biomarker and ethical limitations remain.

Why it matters for you: A broad overview of the neuroplasticity-based antidepressant landscape you work in, useful for framing your fellowship proposals.
