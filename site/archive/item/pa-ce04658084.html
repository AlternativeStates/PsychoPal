Feasibility of Simulated Low-Count [18F]FDG PET/MR for Patients with Pediatric Epilepsy.

RuShuai Li, Feng Wang, Rui Yang, Rui Luo, Xiaoyuan Li, Runze Wu, Jiaxin Hao, Liangshun Xiong, Heping Ju, Shuyue Ai, Lei Xu, Qingle Meng
Journal of nuclear medicine technology · 2026-11-17 · doi:10.2967/jnmt.126.272511
EpilepsyClinicalPeer reviewedPreprocessing / source / toolsMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#pet#low-dose imaging#paediatric epilepsy

Aim To test whether simulated low-count [18F]FDG PET/MR preserves diagnostic quality and epileptogenic zone lateralisation in paediatric epilepsy.

Methods Twenty-seven paediatric patients with epilepsy; list-mode data truncated to simulate 50%, 65% and 80% count reductions, analysed with ROI- and voxel-based lateralisation methods.

Results Contrast-to-noise fell and coefficient of variation rose with count reduction. Image quality stayed clinically acceptable up to 65% reduction, with consistent SUVRs and unchanged lateralisation agreement with the clinical consensus.

Conclusion A 65% simulated count reduction preserved acceptable image quality and lateralisation in paediatric epilepsy, supporting future lower-activity validation studies.

Why it matters for you: Low-dose PET design and [18F]FDG quantification are close to your PET kinetic modelling and PET-MR methods work.
