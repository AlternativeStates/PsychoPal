EXPRESS: Blood-free full quantification of dynamic TSPO PET imaging with [11C]PBR28.

Ludovica Brusaferri, Alexander C Whitehead, Leonardo Barzon, Lucia Maccioni, Zeynab Alshelh, Minhae Kim, Antonio Micoli, Nicola Toschi, Matteo Ferrante, Marianna Inglese, Ciprian Catana, Jodi Gilman, Mattia Veronese, Oliver D Howes, Tiago Marques, Maria Antonietta Nettis, Valeria Mondelli, Carmine M Pariante, Federico E Turkheimer, Enrico Grisan, Marco Loggia
Journal of cerebral blood flow and metabolism : official journal of the International Society of Cerebral Blood Flow and Metabolism · 2026-11-12 · doi:10.1177/0271678x261508659
ClinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#pet#tspo#arterial input function

Aim Whether a deep-learning arterial input function estimate can replace blood sampling for quantitative dynamic TSPO PET with [11C]PBR28.

Methods ASTRA framework developed on 90 participants with arterial data, transfer-learned and tested on 52 at a second centre, applied to a clinical cohort of 32 without arterial data and a blocking cohort of 5.

Results ASTRA-derived VT agreed with arterial measurements (CCC 0.658-0.805). It reproduced SUVR-based group differences using DVR (p<0.001) except in the pituitary gland, and gave receptor occupancy of 0.92 versus 0.94 arterially.

Conclusion The authors conclude multi-site, non-invasive quantitative TSPO PET is feasible, though higher uncertainty accompanies larger error.

Why it matters for you: Directly relevant to your PET kinetic modelling work, offering a route to quantification without arterial sampling.
