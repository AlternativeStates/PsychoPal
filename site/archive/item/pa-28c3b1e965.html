Bayesian Kinetic Model Selection in Dynamic PET via Consistency Models

Yun Zhao, Qinlin Gu, Andrew J. Reader, Yanan Fan, Georgios I. Angelis, Katya Amadita, Gaelle M. Emvalomenos, Evan D. Morris, Guobao Wang, Steven R. Meikle
arXiv · 2026-11-09 · doi:10.48550/arxiv.2611.12235
PETNicotine / smokingPre-clinicalPreprintInformation theory / complexityComputational / whole-brain modellingPreprocessing / source / toolsMachine learning / AIMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#pet kinetic modelling#bayesian#deep learning

Aim Perform voxel-wise Bayesian kinetic model selection and parameter estimation in dynamic PET using consistency models, faster than MCMC.

Methods Consistency model sampling the joint posterior over kinetic parameters and model index, validated in simulations and clinical whole-body [18F]FDG total-body PET and brain [11C]raclopride PET.

Results Model selection accuracy was 97.97% for [18F]FDG (versus 58.67% for NLS+AIC) and 91.4% for [11C]raclopride (versus 73.5% for NLS+BIC), with parameter posteriors closely matching MCMC; smoking-induced dopamine release was mapped in the brain.

Conclusion The authors conclude the framework enables fast, accurate Bayesian kinetic model selection across whole-body and brain PET, though validation is limited to the tracers studied.

Why it matters for you: Directly tied to your PET kinetic modelling work, offering a faster alternative to conventional model selection for voxel-wise analysis.
