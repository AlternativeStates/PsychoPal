Spatiotemporal dynamics of interoceptive conceptual processing in the human brain.

Lucia Amoruso, Agustina Birba, Sebastian Moguilner, Eduardo M Castillo, Tara Kleineschay, Agustín Ibañez, Adolfo M García
Cortex; a journal devoted to the study of the nervous system and behavior · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.1016/j.cortex.2026.10.011
MEGClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformBrain–body physiology#interoception#meg#embodied cognition

Aim How the strength of interoceptive meaning in verbs shapes the spatiotemporal neural dynamics of conceptual processing.

Methods MEG study with 16 participants in a lexical decision task using high- and low-interoceptive verbs; time-frequency analysis, representational similarity analysis, descriptive source reconstruction and cardiac variability measures.

Results High-interoceptive verbs elicited greater low-beta power (15-21 Hz) at 96-176 ms over right temporal sensors, with larger responses linked to lower short-term cardiac variability. Interoceptive strength explained unique representational variance from 190 to 600 ms.

Conclusion Interoceptive strength is a distinct dimension of conceptual organisation, and accessing interoceptive meaning recruits neural dynamics tied to regulating internal bodily states.

Why it matters for you: Combines MEG dynamics with cardiac measures to study interoceptive processing, aligning with your brain-body coupling and neurodynamics interests.
