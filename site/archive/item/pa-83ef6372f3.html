Next generation Human Neocortical Neurosolver model with biophysically realistic somatic, dendritic and synaptic dynamics.

Katharina Duecker, Austin Soplata, Nicholas Tolley, Sarah Pugliese, Taylor A E Jones, Chetan Kandpal, Darcy A Waller, Dylan S Daniels, Stephanie R Jones
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-11-12 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.29.762964
EEGMEGPre-clinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformComputational / whole-brain modellingPreprocessing / source / toolsPre-clinical / invasive#neural modelling#meg#dendritic

Aim To update the Human Neocortical Neurosolver canonical circuit model with electrophysiology and dendritic dynamics tuned to human tissue.

Methods Biophysical multiscale model of L2/3 and L5 pyramidal and interneuron circuits, tuned to human recordings, with simulations of event-related responses and oscillations linked to E/MEG signals.

Results The updated model reproduces realistic somatic dynamics, simulates dendritic calcium spikes in L5 ET neurons and shorter dendritic spikes in L2/3 pyramidal cells, and separates somatic, synaptic and dendritic contributions to E/MEG.

Conclusion The model enables validation against in vitro and in vivo data and gives mechanistic insight into E/MEG signatures.

Why it matters for you: Useful for interpreting your EEG and TMS-EEG results mechanistically and for neural mass modelling alongside DCM work.
