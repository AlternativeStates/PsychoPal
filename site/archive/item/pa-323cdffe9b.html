Negative allosteric modulation of α5-GABAA receptors engages dynamic cortical glutamatergic and GABAergic mechanisms underlying adaptive behavior in mice.

Fernanda Daher, Caio T Fukushima, Jean M Bidlack, John R Atack, Mariana O Popa, Manoela V Fogaça
Neurobiology of disease · 2026-11-23 · doi:10.1016/j.nbd.2026.107690
Ketamine / esketamineDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)Pre-clinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modelling#basmisanil#ketamine-like action#gabaergic interneurons

Aim To test whether negative allosteric modulation of α5-GABAA receptors with basmisanil produces ketamine-like antidepressant actions via specific medial prefrontal cortex cell populations.

Methods Mice exposed to chronic stress; behavioural testing of motivation, hedonia and coping; c-Fos ensemble mapping; signalling and synaptic protein analyses; chemogenetic silencing of CaMKII pyramidal neurons and GABAergic interneurons at different time points.

Results Basmisanil rapidly and durably facilitated behaviour and reversed chronic stress deficits. It preferentially recruited D1R- and SST-expressing cells, increased Erk and Akt-mTOR signalling and synaptic proteins, and early pyramidal silencing blocked both rapid and sustained effects while interneuron silencing blocked sustained effects.

Conclusion Early pyramidal activation initiates rapid plasticity, whereas GABAergic interneurons sustain and express the lasting antidepressant-like effects, highlighting interneuron circuits as targets.

Why it matters for you: Directly relevant to your interest in ketamine-like neuroplastogens and E/I balance, and to circuit-level mechanisms of rapid antidepressant action.
