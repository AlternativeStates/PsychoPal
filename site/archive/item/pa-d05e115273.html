Psychedelic modulation of excitation–inhibition balance and the Affective Core Self: A multilevel mechanistic framework

Michael Winkelman
Journal of Psychedelic Studies · 2026-11-06 · doi:10.1556/2054.2026.00557
Psychedelics (general)fMRIClinicalPeer reviewedConnectivity / synchronyInformation theory / complexityPre-clinical / invasive

Serotonergic psychedelics reliably alter affective tone, emotional regulation, and self-experience. Prevailing theories emphasize acute changes in neural entropy, default mode network integrity, and large-scale functional connectivity but do not offer a clear mechanism that explains how 5-HT2A receptor pharmacology relates to lasting changes in affect and self-related processing.
