Novel quantification method for psilocin in brain tissues using liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry.

Amir Khajavinia, Jordan Reekie, Deborah Michel, Robert B Laprairie, Anas El-Aneed
Analytical methods : advancing methods and applications · 2026-11-18 · doi:10.1039/d6ay01614f
PsilocybinDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)AnxietyPre-clinicalPeer reviewedPreprocessing / source / tools#psilocin#lc-ms/ms#pharmacokinetics

Aim Develops and validates an LC-MS/MS method to quantify psilocin and 4-HIAA in brain tissue.

Methods Chromatography-centred LC-MS/MS approach with matrix dilution, shallower gradient and diverter-valve timing, applied to mouse brain sections from psilocybin and psilocin co-crystal dosing.

Results The method reached a 0.05 ng/mL LLOQ and met validation criteria; no sex or region differences in brain psilocin were found, and psilocybin gave higher psilocin at 10 min than the co-crystal. 4-HIAA was not detected.

Conclusion The validated method enables reliable psilocin quantification in brain tissue, supporting pharmacokinetic work, though the co-crystal difference was not significant.

Why it matters for you: A validated brain-tissue psilocin assay would directly support pharmacokinetic characterisation relevant to your psilocybin and tryptamine work.
