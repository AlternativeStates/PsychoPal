Nitric oxide feedback to ciliary photoreceptor cells gates a UV avoidance circuit.

Kei Jokura, Piotr Slowinski, Nobuo Ueda, Martin Gühmann, Luis A Yañez Guerra, Emily Savage, Kyle C A Wedgwood, Gáspár Jékely
eLife · 2026-11-24 · doi:10.7554/elife.91258
Peer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingBrain stimulation#nitric oxide#photoreceptors#circuit gating

Aim How nitric oxide signalling gates a UV-avoidance circuit in the annelid larva Platynereis dumerilii.

Methods Genetic mutants, behavioural assays, cell-specific expression of nitric oxide synthase, and mathematical modelling of phototransduction and circuit dynamics.

Results UV stimulation activated nitric oxide production in interneurons, which signalled retrogradely to ciliary photoreceptors and sustained their activation. NOS mutants showed defective retrograde signalling, ciliary activation and UV avoidance.

Conclusion Retrograde nitric oxide signalling gates a synaptic circuit and provides short-term memory of UV exposure to guide avoidance behaviour.

Why it matters for you: Retrograde gaseous signalling and circuit gating in a simple photoreceptive system is relevant to your interest in neuromodulation and dynamics.
