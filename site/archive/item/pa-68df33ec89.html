A General-Purpose Adaptable Foundation Model for Total-Body PET Kinetic Modelling.

Yun Zhao, Qinlin Gu, Andrew J Reader, Yanan Fan, Georgios I Angelis, Katya Amadita, Kevin J Chung, Xiaoyu Duan, Benjamin A Spencer, Guobao Wang, Steven R Meikle
ArXiv · 2026-10-30
PETClinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingMachine learning / AIMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)

Background Voxel-level kinetic modeling in total-body dynamic positron emission tomography (TB-PET) enables precise physiological quantification but complicates parameter uncertainty estimation.

Methods To address this, we introduce LoRACM, a parameter-efficient framework utilizing low-rank adaptation (LoRA) to transfer a pretrained foundation CM to novel TB-PET scenarios.

Results In human data, LoRACM generated total-body K_i maps comparable to the ones made by non-negative nonlinear least squares (NNLS) fitting and a consistency model trained from scratch. Striatal dopamine binding potential estimates predicted by LoRACM correlated with NNLS estimates (r = 0.968-0.988).

Conclusion These underscore the potential of adapting a pretrained CM with LoRA for fast and reliable posterior parameter inference for kinetic models across distinct TB-PET applications.
