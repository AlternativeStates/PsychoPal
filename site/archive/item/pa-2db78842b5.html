5-HT2A receptor deletion is associated with reduced hippocampal parvalbumin levels and anxiety-like behavior in BALB/c mice

K. Dial, P. Wonnenberg, J. Ma, K. Conant
biorxiv (neuroscience) · 2026-11-20 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.19.767898
AnxietyPre-clinicalPreprintPre-clinical / invasive#5-ht2a#parvalbumin#anxiety

Aim Investigates whether constitutive loss of 5-HT2A receptors alters anxiety-like behaviour and hippocampal parvalbumin interneuron proteins in BALB/c mice.

Methods Constitutive 5-HT2AR knockout in male and female BALB/c mice, behavioural anxiety paradigms, unbiased hippocampal proteomics, and perineuronal net component analysis; a separate cohort received the 5-HT2AR agonist 25CN-NBOH.

Results Knockout mice showed robust anxiety-like and repetitive behaviour, with reduced hippocampal parvalbumin and energy-metabolism proteins, and reduced HAPLN4, while aggrecan and HAPLN1 were unchanged.

Conclusion Loss of 5-HT2AR signalling is associated with coordinated reductions in parvalbumin and bioenergetic machinery without generalised loss of perineuronal net components.

Why it matters for you: Connects 5-HT2A signalling to parvalbumin interneurons, relevant to the anxiolytic mechanisms of psychedelics you study.
