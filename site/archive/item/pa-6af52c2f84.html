iEEG Can Help EEG: Improving Clinical Scalp EEG Analysis through Intracranial Priors

Xianglong Bao, Yi Ding, Zengxiang Li, Nan Liu, Kewen Wang, Zhe Wang, Ming Jin
arXiv (q-bio.NC) · 2026-11-16 · doi:10.48550/arxiv.2611.20435
EEGClinicalPreprintComputational / whole-brain modellingPre-clinical / invasive

Background Scalp electroencephalography (EEG) is a cornerstone of clinical brain monitoring, making the quality of its signal fundamental to downstream tasks and models.

Results ReBrain consistently improves host models across recordings from multiple hospitals and across architecture families, and our analysis traces this gain to genuine neural structure carried by iEEG rather than to the architecture itself.

Conclusion This work provides evidence that a scarce clean modality can serve as a transferable prior for an abundant noisy one, offering a general recipe for cross-modal transfer among biosignals.
