Psychedelic assisted psychotherapy (2020-2025): a scoping review of clinical evidence, mechanisms, safety, and ethics.

Aim To map clinical evidence, mechanisms, safety and ethics of psychedelic-assisted therapy published 2020-2025.

Methods Scoping review of PubMed, PsycINFO, Scopus and ClinicalTrials.gov searched 23 February 2026, covering psilocybin, MDMA, LSD and DMT studies.

Results The text provided is truncated and does not report specific findings.

Conclusion The review emphasises set and setting, and methodological, ethical and implementation considerations across protocols.

Why it matters for you: A broad overview of the psychedelic-assisted therapy evidence base that frames your clinical pharmacology and psilocybin depression work.
