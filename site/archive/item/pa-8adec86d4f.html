Quantitative Analysis of Lysergic Acid Diethylamide: A Ring Study Among Five Swiss Drug Checking Services.

Manuela Carla Monti, Emilia Gianola, Raoul P J Koning, Mireia Ventura, Pieter E Oomen
Drug testing and analysis · 2026-11-14 · doi:10.1002/dta.70190
LSDPeer reviewedInformation theory / complexityClinical trial / evidence synthesis#lsd#drug checking#analytical chemistry

Aim Whether LSD quantification across Swiss drug checking laboratories is accurate and reproducible.

Methods Interlaboratory ring trial across five Swiss drug checking services using HPLC-DAD, GC-MS and HPLC-MS/MS on two LSD-infused blotters and one methanolic solution, with results corrected by qNMR-determined purity.

Results Relative standard deviations were 8%–16.5% and mean accuracy −5.3% to −13.0%, with more variability for the liquid sample and the largest deviations from mass spectrometry methods.

Conclusion The authors conclude current LSD quantification is reliable in drug checking settings and provide a basis for future proficiency testing, noting stability and production factors as possible sources of discrepancy.

Why it matters for you: Peripheral to your PET and neuroimaging work, though it bears on dose accuracy in psychedelic research and harm reduction.
