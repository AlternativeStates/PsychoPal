3p-C-NETA: A Versatile Theranostic Chelator Platform Enabling Al18F- and 177Lu-Labeled SSTR2-Targeted Radioligands.

Ward Storms, Stephen Ahenkorah, Danni Ramdhani, Christophe M Deroose, Thomas Cardinaels, Guy Bormans, Hiroshi Watabe, Maarten Ooms, Janke Kleynhans, Frederik Cleeren
Pharmaceutics · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.3390/pharmaceutics18111414
PETPre-clinicalPeer reviewedPre-clinical / invasiveMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#radiochemistry#theranostics#pet

Aim To evaluate 3p-C-NETA-TATE as a theranostic precursor that supports both Al18F and lutetium-177 labelling for SSTR2-targeted radioligands.

Methods The precursor was labelled with Al18F or 177Lu; the two radioligands were compared in vitro for binding affinity, ex vivo biodistribution, and PET imaging in tumour-bearing mice, and 177Lu-3p-C-NETA-TATE was compared with 177Lu-DOTA-TATE for labelling, stability and cytotoxicity.

Results 177Lu labelling exceeded 95% at lower temperature and precursor concentration than DOTA-TATE, though serum stability was reduced. The Al18F and 177Lu versions showed similar biological profiles.

Conclusion The 3p-C-NETA platform shows theranostic potential as a versatile chelator for diagnostic and therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals.

Why it matters for you: Radiochemistry for Al18F and 177Lu labelling is only loosely connected to your PET imaging work, mainly through the shared radiotracer methods.
