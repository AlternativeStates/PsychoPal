Neural Entropy Pattern : An EEG Representation for Predicting Depression Therapy Outcomes.

Hesam Akbari, Hamed Shamsi, Tayyaba Shahwar, Niloofar Alizadeh, Muhammad Tariq Sadiq, Hana Rostami, Reza Kazemi, Siuly Siuly, Mutlu Mete
Biomedical physics & engineering express · 2026-11-19 · doi:10.1088/2057-1976/aec455
EEGDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalPeer reviewedInformation theory / complexityComputational / whole-brain modellingPreprocessing / source / toolsMachine learning / AI#eeg complexity#treatment response#entropy

Aim Whether a multichannel EEG entropy representation can predict antidepressant and rTMS treatment response before treatment begins.

Methods Neural Entropy Pattern of ten entropy measures across 19 channels and five bands plus broadband; twelve deep and seven shallow models tested on SSRI (n = 30) and rTMS (n = 61) datasets under LOSO and five-fold cross-validation.

Results Best balanced accuracy was 76.4% for SSRI under LOSO (AUC 0.89) and 62.1% to 63.7% for rTMS (AUC 0.62 to 0.63). Models were selected post hoc, with wide confidence intervals, and only the SSRI results were above chance by AUC.

Conclusion Entropy-based EEG features show some pre-treatment predictive value, mainly for SSRI response. The rTMS result is weaker and the selection was post hoc.

Why it matters for you: Pre-treatment EEG prediction of response is directly relevant to your interest in neurodynamics and complexity measures.
