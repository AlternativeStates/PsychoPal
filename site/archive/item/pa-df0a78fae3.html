Obesity pharmacotherapies and cognition

Stina Börchers, Giulia Mazzini, Henrik Zetterberg, Christoffer Clemmensen
Science · 2026-11-05 · doi:10.1126/science.aek9987
Peer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingClinical trial / evidence synthesis

Pharmacotherapies for obesity and type 2 diabetes are increasingly being evaluated for their effects on cognition and brain health. Evidence from preclinical models, observational cohorts, and clinical trials has intensified interest, but findings remain mixed.
