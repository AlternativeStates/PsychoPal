Cortical dynamics during psychedelic and anesthetized states induced by ketamine

Duan Li, George A. Mashour
NeuroImage · 2019-04-05 · doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.03.076
Ketamine / esketamineMEGTMS / TMS-EEGHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformERP / MMN / predictive codingInformation theory / complexityComputational / whole-brain modellingBrain stimulationMachine learning / AI#ketamine#eeg complexity#consciousness

Aim To characterise how the spatiotemporal complexity of EEG activity changes across subanesthetic and anaesthetic doses of ketamine.

Methods High-density scalp EEG in healthy volunteers during subanesthetic and anaesthetic ketamine. Hidden Markov modelling classified brain states, and Lempel-Ziv complexity was computed per state after controlling for spectral changes.

Results Subanesthetic ketamine raised complexity relative to baseline. Anaesthetic ketamine produced alternating low and high complexity before stabilising at a high level comparable to baseline.

Conclusion Ketamine-induced state transitions share features of anaesthesia, normal consciousness and altered consciousness, illustrating its complex pharmacological properties.

Why it matters for you: Closely matches your ketamine EEG work and your interest in Lempel-Ziv complexity and criticality as markers of altered states.
