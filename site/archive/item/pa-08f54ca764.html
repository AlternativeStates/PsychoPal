Introducing the “ASCENT” EEGLAB Plugin: Aperiodic Signal Complexity Estimation for Neurophysiological Time Series

Cédric Cannard, Arnaud Delorme
Entropy · 2026-11-24 · doi:10.3390/e28121301
EEGMEGClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformInformation theory / complexityPreprocessing / source / tools#eeg complexity#aperiodic activity#eeglab

Aim Introduce ASCENT, an open-source EEGLAB plugin for computing entropy, fractal and aperiodic measures on neurophysiological time series.

Methods Software toolbox combining single-scale and multiscale entropy, multivariate entropy, fractal dimension and aperiodic spectral parameterisation, applied to independent components and channels; example on resting-state EEG from 40 participants, eyes closed and open.

Results Condition differences appeared for several measures and scales, with direction and spatial distribution depending on the measure, coarse-graining operator and aperiodic correction. Correlation analyses identified redundant measures.

Conclusion ASCENT aims to make these complexity measures easier to apply consistently and interpret appropriately.

Why it matters for you: Directly relevant to your EEG complexity, criticality and aperiodic activity work, offering a standardised pipeline you could adopt.
