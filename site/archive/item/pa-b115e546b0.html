Consciousness Beyond the Brain: The Role of the Autonomic Nervous System in Non-ordinary States of Consciousness

Lana Porter, Valerie Bonnelle, Marieke Schölvinck, Martha Havenith
PsyArXiv · 2026-11-17 · doi:10.31234/osf.io/5u7sy_v3
Psychedelics (general)PreprintBrain–body physiology#autonomic nervous system#non-ordinary states#interoception

Aim What role the autonomic nervous system plays in shaping non-ordinary states of consciousness such as psychedelic, breathwork and hypnotic states.

Methods Narrative review of autonomic contributions to NOSCs, linking autonomic responses to cortical dynamics and proposing a framework.

Conclusion The authors propose that physiological stress in a supportive context enables NOSCs, with combined sympathetic and parasympathetic tone marking peak states. They suggest autonomic monitoring could make induction safer and more effective.

Why it matters for you: Links autonomic physiology to psychedelic states, bridging your interests in brain-body coupling and psychedelic research.
