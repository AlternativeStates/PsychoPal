Longitudinal plasma biomarkers and multimodal PET across APOE ε4 genotypes: a prospective cohort study

Mikko Koivumäki, Anniina Snellman, Laura L. Ekblad, Andrea Benedet, Henrik Zetterberg, Juha O. Rinne
Research Square · 2026-11-01 · doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-11294615/v1
Dementia / Alzheimer'sClinicalPreprintComputational / whole-brain modellingMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#plasma biomarkers#synaptic density pet#apoe

Aim To characterise longitudinal plasma biomarker changes and their links to amyloid, synaptic density and neuroinflammation PET across APOE ε4 genotypes in cognitively unimpaired older adults.

Methods Plasma p-tau217, GFAP, NfL, SNAP25 and NRGN measured by NULISA at baseline (n = 60) and after 4.4 years (n = 45), with linear mixed-effects models and multiple regression against PiB, PK11195 and UCB-J PET.

Results GFAP rose over time in all genotype groups and NfL rose in the ε4/ε4 and ε3/ε3 groups, with no group differences in rate of change. Plasma p-tau217 was the only marker robustly associated with amyloid at both timepoints, while synaptic markers showed minimal change and no association with synaptic density PET.

Conclusion Plasma p-tau217 tracks amyloid-driven pathology specifically, while neurodegeneration markers reflect broader ageing not linked to APOE genotype.
