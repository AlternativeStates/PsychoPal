The Forgotten Search for an LSD "Antidote".

Roberto De Filippo
Psychedelic medicine (New Rochelle, N.Y.) · 2026-11-14 · doi:10.1177/28314425261507297
LSDPeer reviewedPre-clinical / invasive#lsd#chlorpromazine#history

Aim To reconstruct a neglected 1950s finding that chlorpromazine partially antagonises LSD effects, and to translate the foundational 1955 study.

Methods Historical commentary with the first English translation of Giberti and Gregoretti (1955), set alongside CIA MKULTRA-funded human experimentation and modern receptor pharmacology.

Conclusion Chlorpromazine's antagonism of LSD is explained by its 5-HT2A receptor antagonism, so contemporary receptor knowledge validates these early clinical observations.

Why it matters for you: Gives historical and receptor-level context for LSD drug-drug interactions, relevant to your psychedelic pharmacology work.
