Classic psychedelics and the neuroendocrine system: A systematic review of human studies.

Taini Bellucci, Caroline Carvalho Gomes, José Carlos Bouso, Jaime E C Hallak, Rafael G Dos Santos
Journal of psychopharmacology (Oxford, England) · 2026-11-25 · doi:10.1177/02698811261504429
PsilocybinLSDDMTAyahuascaMescalinePeer reviewedClinical trial / evidence synthesis#psychedelics#neuroendocrine#systematic review

Aim Systematically review human studies of neuroendocrine responses to classic psychedelics.

Methods PRISMA-guided systematic review of PubMed, LILACS, Cochrane and Web of Science for studies from 1990 to July 2026, extracting substance, dose, sample, sampling timing and hormonal effects.

Results Results are not reported in the supplied text; the abstract describes the search and extraction protocol only.

Conclusion Endocrine effects of classic psychedelics remain understudied despite therapeutic interest, and the review aims to map the existing human evidence.

Why it matters for you: Relevant to the stress, homeostasis and hormonal side of psychedelic pharmacology that your clinical work touches.
