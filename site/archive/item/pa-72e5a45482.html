Linguistic contextualization in the human hippocampus.

Kalman A Katlowitz, James L Belanger, Taha Ismail, Ana G Chavez, Assia Chericoni, Melissa Franch, Elizabeth A Mickiewicz, Raissa K Mathura, Alica M Goldman, Vaishnav Krishnan, Atul Maheshwari, Mohammed Hasen, Danika L Paulo, Andrew J Watrous, Eleonora Bartoli, Steven T Piantadosi, Nicole R Provenza, Sameer A Sheth, Benjamin Y Hayden
Nature human behaviour · 2026-11-23 · doi:10.1038/s41562-026-02543-z
ClinicalPeer reviewedMachine learning / AI

Background Word meanings in language are contextualized by surrounding words.

Methods We analysed single unit activity in the human hippocampus, a region involved in semantic and contextual processing, while n = 10 participants listened to podcasts.

Results We found that hippocampal neurons encoded word position within a clause, using both ordinal and frequency-domain positional encoding. The relative weighting of these contextualizing words correlated with LLM self-attention weights.
