OSS-DBS v2: Simulation Platform for Biophysical Modelling of Deep Brain Stimulation.

Julius Zimmermann, Jan Philipp Payonk, Lam Vien Che, Joachim Schöberl, Till A Dembek, Andreas Horn, Ursula van Rienen, Konstantin Butenko
medRxiv : the preprint server for health sciences · 2026-10-13 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.10.26365197
Pre-clinicalPeer reviewedConnectivity / synchronyComputational / whole-brain modellingBrain stimulation#deep brain stimulation#biophysical modelling#open-source

Aim To present OSS-DBS v2, an open-source platform for biophysical modelling of deep brain stimulation.

Methods Software combining automated volume conductor generation, electric field modelling and stimulation-driven neural simulation, with an electrode library, API, Lead-DBS integration and an optimisation framework.

Results Not reported in the abstract; the platform is described as supporting all major operating systems and running on consumer-grade hardware.

Conclusion The authors present OSS-DBS v2 as a fully open-source tool for multi-modal DBS modelling by researchers and clinicians.

Why it matters for you: Biophysical stimulation modelling is adjacent to your tDCS and TMS interests, though DBS targets are outside your current research.
