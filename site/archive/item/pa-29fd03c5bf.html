NeuroSim: a clinically informed multi-disorder EEG simulator with personalized pattern injection and AI-driven neurological modeling

Chayut Bunterngchit, Saba Aslam, Aamir Sattar, Abdur Rasool, Yishan Wang
bioRxiv · 2026-11-20 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.19.767788
EEGPsychosis / schizophreniaEpilepsyDementia / Alzheimer'sParkinson'sClinicalPreprintComputational / whole-brain modellingMachine learning / AI

Background Deep learning for electroencephalography (EEG) based neurodiagnostics is constrained by the limited availability of labeled clinical recordings.

Methods Existing EEG simulators typically address a single disorder or trade biological interpretability for statistical fidelity.

Results A convolutional-attention classifier trained on simulated data reached 95.3 to 96.8 percent hold-out accuracy, and post hoc explainability analyses produced class-separable embeddings with attributions concentrated on pathophysiologically expected electrodes.

Conclusion NeuroSim is released as open-source software to support augmentation, benchmarking, and explainable artificial intelligence in neurological diagnostics.
