Spectral and complexity-based signatures of advanced concentration absorption meditation: A high-density EEG study of jhana

Peter Moltke Skov-Andersen, Remko van Lutterveld, Sofie Paludanus, Thomas Cahaly, Ruby M. Potash, Andrés Canales‐Johnson, Winson Fu Zun Yang, Matthew D. Sacchet
bioRxiv (Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory) · 2026-11-18 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.15.766880
PreprintSpectral / aperiodic / waveformInformation theory / complexityComputational / whole-brain modelling

Background Advanced meditation provides structured and reproducible alterations of consciousness that are increasingly used as model states for the neuroscience of consciousness.

Methods We applied high-density EEG and a comprehensive multidimensional battery of spectral and complexity measures to characterize each ACAM-J state individually in 20 advanced meditators, together with phenomenological ratings.

Results ACAM-J produced widespread posterior-central reductions in oscillatory and aperiodic power, posterior aperiodic flattening across all eight states, and convergent increases in entropy-based complexity over the posterior cortex, with power effects strengthening with phenomenological depth.

Conclusion ACAM-J thus emerges as a non-pharmacological model state at the high-complexity end of the spectrum of altered consciousness, and reveals a previously unquantified dissociation between neural complexity and experiential richness for specific ACAM-J states.
