Radiopharmaceuticals in Molecular Neuroimaging: Radiotracers in Development.

Kota Yokoyama, Ana M Franceschi, Gloria Chia-Yi Chiang, Kenji Ishii, Kimiteru Ito
Neuroimaging clinics of North America · 2026-09-30 · doi:10.1016/j.nic.2026.07.009
PETClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#radiotracers#molecular neuroimaging#sv2a pet

Aim Review emerging radiopharmaceuticals for molecular neuroimaging, organised by target biology and translational readiness.

Methods Narrative review of developing agents including second-generation tau, alpha-synuclein, SV2A, TSPO and non-TSPO inflammation, amino acid and neuro-oncology tracers, and hypoxia/redox theranostics.

Results The review describes clinical use cases, interpretive pitfalls, regional approval differences and implementation barriers across these platforms.

Conclusion The authors conclude that clinical value depends on reproducible quantification as well as lesion visibility.

Why it matters for you: Useful overview of the tracer landscape, including SV2A synaptic density agents close to your PET work.
