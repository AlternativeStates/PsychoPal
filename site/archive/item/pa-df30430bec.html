Multidimensional Electrophysiological Responses to TMS on the Primary Motor Cortex: A TMS-EEG Characterization of Immediate Cortical Responses

Yuyao He, Wei Wang, Dechen Kong, Jiayu Chen, Hongying Li, Haoyang Xing, Hongmei Zhang
Bioengineering · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.3390/bioengineering13111298
EEGTMS / TMS-EEGHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformERP / MMN / predictive codingBrain stimulation#tms-eeg#neuromodulation#tep

Aim Compare immediate cortical responses to 10 Hz rTMS, 1 Hz rTMS and iTBS over the primary motor cortex using TMS-EEG.

Methods Randomised within-subject crossover study in 17 healthy adults receiving three stimulation protocols in separate sessions, with TEPs, power spectral density and EEG microstate dynamics assessed before and after stimulation.

Results After 10 Hz rTMS, P30 amplitude rose from 0.17 to 0.89 μV and PSD increased across 15.5–45 Hz. After 1 Hz rTMS, P30 fell and P60 rose, with PSD reductions. iTBS reduced N45 and P60 magnitudes.

Conclusion The authors conclude that each protocol produces distinct multidimensional electrophysiological profiles, supporting systematic protocol comparison and individualised neuromodulation.
