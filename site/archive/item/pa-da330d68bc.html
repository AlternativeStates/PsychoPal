From Evidence to Practice: A Systematic Review of Implementation of Psychedelic-Assisted Psychotherapy.

John D Gault, Erin Almklov, Shirley Tang, Borsika A Rabin, Brian C Huynh, James O E Pittman
Psychedelic medicine (New Rochelle, N.Y.) · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.1177/28314425261500673
Psychedelics (general)Depression (incl. TRD/MDD)Peer reviewedClinical trial / evidence synthesis

Aim The specific objectives of this systematic review were to: (1) identify extant literature on implementation of PAP, and (2) synthesize and present findings to understand current implementation knowledge and inform future directions.

Methods This systematic review followed the procedures outlined in Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses 2020.

Results A total of 2,406 articles were identified, and 2,074 of these were screened by title and abstract after the initial elimination of duplicates and sources that were not peer-reviewed research articles (e.g., gray literature). Forty-seven were retrieved for full text review, and 20 studies were included in the review.

Conclusion There is a noticeable gap in implementation-focused research on best practices for translating PAP from academic settings into accessible clinical programs.
