Practicing Focused Attention or Open Monitoring During Interoceptive Challenges: Seed-Based Functional Connectivity Changes Following Video-Based Online MBSR.

Jun-Ding Zhu, Ai-Ling Hsu, Kai-Jie Liang, Changwei W Wu, Shwu-Hua Lee, Pei-Ying Sarah Chan
Biomedical journal · 2026-11-14 · doi:10.1016/j.bj.2026.101068
fMRIHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewedConnectivity / synchrony#mindfulness#interoception#fmri

Aim How eight weeks of video-based online MBSR changes functional connectivity of the dorsal attention and salience networks during interoceptive challenges.

Methods 39 healthy adults (18 MBSR, 21 control) with fMRI before and after the intervention during inspiratory occlusions under baseline, focused attention and open monitoring; seed-based connectivity from frontal eye fields and anterior cingulate cortex.

Results An exploratory Group by Time interaction in anterior cingulate connectivity during open monitoring, with a greater negative change to the right frontal pole in the MBSR group; this did not survive correction across all interaction analyses. MBSR also changed frontal eye field and anterior cingulate connectivity within the group.

Conclusion Connectivity between network seeds and cortical regions changed after online MBSR; the group difference needs replication and behavioural relevance remains to be tested.

Why it matters for you: Examines salience network connectivity during controlled breathing-based interoceptive load, touching your brain-body and interoception interests.
