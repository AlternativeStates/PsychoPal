Cortical depth EEG-fMRI at 7T: Experimental setup and analysis pipeline to elucidate generating mechanisms of alpha oscillations.

Daniel C Marsh, Rodika Sokoliuk, Kevin M Aquino, Daisie O Pakenham, Ross Wilson, Rosa Sanchez Panchuelo, Matthew J Brookes, Simon Hanslmayr, Stephen D Mayhew, Susan T Francis, Karen J Mullinger
Imaging neuroscience (Cambridge, Mass.) · 2026-11-06 · doi:10.1162/imag.a.1442
EEGfMRIClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformPre-clinical / invasive#alpha oscillations#eeg-fmri#laminar fmri

Aim To develop a non-invasive 7T EEG-fMRI framework for studying the generating mechanisms of alpha oscillations across cortical depth.

Methods Simultaneous 7T laminar fMRI and EEG in 10 participants during an eyes-closed/eyes-open paradigm, with a deveining procedure to reduce pial-vein BOLD contamination.

Results In 9 of 10 participants, EEG alpha power correlated negatively with visual cortex BOLD. After deveining, correlations were weaker in middle depths than superficial and deep layers, and this depth dependence disappeared with a boxcar regressor.

Conclusion The authors state the depth profiles are consistent with top-down feedback activity, but causality cannot be definitively demonstrated from these data.

Why it matters for you: Directly relevant to your EEG and neurodynamics interests, offering a laminar fMRI approach to oscillation origins.
