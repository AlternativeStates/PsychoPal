Hallucinogenic Mechanisms of Psychedelics: From Molecular Pharmacology to Predictive Processing

Open-access 29-page monograph synthesising evidence on how classical serotonergic, dissociative and atypical hallucinogens alter perception and cognition, mapping molecular actions to predictive processing models such as REBUS. Verification notes flag contested claims, including direct TrkB binding.

Why it matters for you: Synthesises the 5-HT2A and predictive-processing literature that bears on your neuroplastogen and neurodynamics interests, though it is a single-author, self-published overview.
