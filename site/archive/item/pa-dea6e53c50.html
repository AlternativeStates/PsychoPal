Cross-cohort validation of EEG biomarkers using a pre-registered three-criterion framework: a demonstration in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis

B. T. Gebregergis, H. G. Yhdego, T. Teklu
medrxiv (neurology) · 2026-11-12 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.10.26367741
Healthy volunteersClinicalPreprintSpectral / aperiodic / waveformInformation theory / complexityPreprocessing / source / tools#eeg biomarkers#als#reliability

Aim To develop and demonstrate a pre-registered three-criterion framework for identifying EEG features that show discrimination, reliability and cross-cohort consistency in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis.

Methods Eighteen EEG features assessed across three cohorts: a home-recorded ALS cohort with 170 controls, a clinic-recorded ALS cohort of eight patients, and a healthy test-retest reference of 60 participants, with permutation and bootstrap robustness testing.

Results Gamma power, theta power and spectral entropy met all three criteria. Cross-cohort differences in reliability, such as relative beta power (ICC 0.09 at home versus 0.93 in clinic), were large relative to the disease signal, and the selected set was sensitive to sample composition.

Conclusion Recording setting and protocol differences can outweigh disease-associated EEG signal, so cross-cohort validation is essential before biomarker translation.

Why it matters for you: Speaks to your EEG work on neurodynamics and reliability, and to the methodological care needed for EEG biomarkers.
