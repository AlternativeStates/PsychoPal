Monte Carlo simulation of a neuroimaging acquisition with MAPSSIC, aβ+sensitive intracerebral microprobe for awake and freely moving rats.

S El Ketara, F Agnese, L Ammour, C Bouillot, S Bouvard, O Clausse, M Dupont, F Gensolen, M Goffe, M Kachel, J Laurence, P Pangaud, C Wabnitz, T Weicherding, J Baudot, P Lanièce, C Morel, L Zimmer, M-A Verdier
Physics in medicine and biology · 2026-11-27 · doi:10.1088/1361-6560/aebdfb
Pre-clinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingPreprocessing / source / toolsMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#beta-plus probe#raclopride#preclinical pet

Aim Evaluate by Monte Carlo simulation whether an implantable beta-plus sensitive probe can quantify binding in awake, freely moving rats.

Methods Monte Carlo simulation of an in vivo [11C]raclopride experiment using the MAPSSIC pixelated probe, compared with micro-PET for non-displaceable binding potential.

Results Over 93% of detected events came from within 2 mm of the probe. Variations of 5–30% in BPND were quantified with under 4% discrepancy from micro-PET.

Conclusion The authors conclude the probe can capture small localised binding variations, supporting its use for quantitative kinetic analysis in structures such as the striatum, pending experimental validation.

Why it matters for you: Directly relevant to your PET kinetic modelling and interest in linking molecular imaging to behaviour in animals.
