Paired-Pulse TMS as a Window into Cortical Microcircuits: Mechanistic Insights and Translational Challenges

Pietro Antenucci, João Moura, Maria Lucrezia Lavena, Ricci Hannah, Anna Latorre, Lorenzo Rocchi
Biomolecules · 2026-11-09 · doi:10.3390/biom16111633
TMS / TMS-EEGClinicalPeer reviewedERP / MMN / predictive codingConnectivity / synchronyComputational / whole-brain modellingBrain stimulationPre-clinical / invasive

Background Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) generates cortical electrical currents that, when applied to the primary motor cortex, depolarise neurons, producing motor evoked potentials.

Methods Despite decades of use, clinical translation of these protocols is limited. Interpretation is constrained by reliance on in vivo and pharmacological models, which only partially capture human cortical microcircuitry.
