Which Psychedelic Therapy Should Rural Health Systems Pursue First: Psilocybin or Ibogaine?

Todd Davies, Alberto Coustasse
The Permanente Journal · 2026-11-05 · doi:10.7812/tpp/26.139
PsilocybinIbogainePeer reviewedBrain–body physiology#psilocybin#ibogaine#rural health

Aim Whether rural health systems should prioritise psilocybin or ibogaine for investment planning in psychedelic-assisted therapy.

Methods Point-counterpoint commentary comparing psilocybin and ibogaine from a rural health system perspective, covering evidence, regulation, cost, access and cardiac risk.

Conclusion Psilocybin is further developed for conditional implementation, while ibogaine merits research partnerships with monitoring given cardiac risk and limited controlled evidence; a phased approach is suggested.

Why it matters for you: Policy-level context on psilocybin versus ibogaine, useful when framing your psychedelic clinical pharmacology proposals.
