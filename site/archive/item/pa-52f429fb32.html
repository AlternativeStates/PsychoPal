HHS announces new funding opportunities for psychedelics, with focus on ibogaine

Alison Knopf
Alcoholism & Drug Abuse Weekly · 2026-11-20 · doi:10.1002/adaw.35123
PsilocybinIbogaineKetamine / esketaminePTSDPeer reviewedConnectivity / synchrony#funding#psychedelics#ibogaine

HHS announced a $20 million NIMH funding opportunity for a research network on rapid-acting psychotropic drugs such as ketamine, esketamine and psilocybin, plus up to $4.9 million via ARPA-H for ibogaine development in PTSD and other serious mental illnesses.

Why it matters for you: Signals new US funding for rapid-acting psychotropics and ibogaine, relevant to your psychedelic clinical pharmacology plans and postdoctoral applications.
