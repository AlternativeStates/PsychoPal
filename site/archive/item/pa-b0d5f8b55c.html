Nogo-related frontal theta-band activity after magnetic seizure therapy in treatment-resistant depression: an exploratory task-based EEG study.

Lu Yuan, Fan Chang, Chenghui Yang, Cui Yuan, Bo Zhou, Yuejia Luo, Ruifeng Shi, Yihan Su, Xue Xia, Ying He
Frontiers in psychiatry · 2026-10-28 · doi:10.3389/fpsyt.2026.1950544
Depression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalPeer reviewedBrain stimulationClinical trial / evidence synthesis

Background However, its neurophysiological mechanisms-particularly those underlying conflict monitoring and response inhibition-remain unclear.

Methods In this open-label, single-arm trial, 19 patients with TRD (16 female; mean age 20.89 ± 8.16 years; 10 of the 19 participants were minors) received 6-12 MST sessions using 100-Hz double-cone coil stimulation targeting …

Results Following MST, patients showed significant reductions in depressive symptoms (HAMD: 40.53 ± 10.25 to 22.53 ± 11.21; MADRS: 35.68 ± 8.84 to 20.68 ± 11.12). At an uncorrected threshold, total Nogo-related theta-band activity (4-7 Hz) at Fz and FCz was lower after treatment; however, these effects did not survive FDR correction.

Conclusion These preliminary findings suggest that MST was associated with short-term symptom improvement and lower total Nogo-related theta-band activity at an uncorrected threshold.
