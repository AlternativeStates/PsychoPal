The application of transcranial magnetic stimulation in treatment-resistant depression: insights from omics.

Yuan Yang, Xiaolina Zhang, Yushan Duan, Jie Wu, Mengyuan Zhang, Ruize Niu, Tianhao Bao
Frontiers in psychiatry · 2026-10-20 · doi:10.3389/fpsyt.2026.1938107
TMS / TMS-EEGDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalPeer reviewedBrain stimulation

Treatment-resistant depression (TRD) is a refractory form of major depressive disorder (MDD) for which conventional antidepressant strategies are often insufficient. Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) has demonstrated clinical efficacy in TRD, but its biological mechanisms remain incompletely understood.
