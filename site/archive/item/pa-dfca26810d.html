Efficacy, location and kinetics at the 5-HT₂A receptor: A narrative review and testable framework for psychoplastogen design.

Satyabrat Biswal, Debadash Panigrahi, Mrityunjay Banerjee, Prakash Chandra Senapati, Prasanna Kumar Pradhan, Pratikeswar Panda, Satyanarayan Pattnaik, Sidhanta Kumar Pani, Laxmidhar Maharana, Durga Madhab Kar
Progress in neuro-psychopharmacology & biological psychiatry · 2026-11-26 · doi:10.1016/j.pnpbp.2026.112026
Non-hallucinogenic analoguesDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modelling#psychoplastogens#5-ht2a#medicinal chemistry

Aim Propose a hypothesis-generating framework for designing psychoplastogens that separate neuroplasticity from hallucinogenic effects at the 5-HT2A receptor.

Methods Narrative review of 5-HT2A efficacy, location and kinetics, TrkB binding claims and medicinal chemistry examples, culminating in a proposed set of components with candidate readouts and falsification criteria.

Results Evidence for TrkB-mediated plasticity is disputed and largely preclinical, and no quantitative structure-activity relationship is asserted. The authors propose the term spatiotemporal signalling phenotype with testable components.

Conclusion The framework is a hypothesis-generating medicinal chemistry proposal, not a validated mechanism or a causal model of antidepressant action.

Why it matters for you: Directly addresses psychoplastogen design and 5-HT2A signalling, matching your interests in neuroplastogens and tryptamine chemistry.
