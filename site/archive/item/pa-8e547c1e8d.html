Lilium–egg yolk composite granules ameliorates depressive-like behaviors in CUMS rats with associated changes in neuroinflammation, gut microbiota, and SCFAs

Yu Huang, Deng Qibo, Jiajia Ji, Sainan Tian, Qinghua Peng, Zhiying Yuan
Frontiers in Nutrition · 2026-11-23 · doi:10.3389/fnut.2026.2003851
SSRIs / antidepressantsDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)Pre-clinicalPeer reviewedBrain–body physiology#gut microbiota#depression#short-chain fatty acids

Aim Assess whether lilium-egg yolk composite granules (BHT) ameliorate depressive-like behaviour in chronically stressed rats through gut microbiota and fatty acid changes.

Methods 72 male SD rats randomised to control, CUMS, three BHT doses or fluoxetine for 28 days; behavioural tests, histology, electron microscopy, cytokine assays, faecal metagenomics and GC-MS of short-chain fatty acids.

Results BHT reduced forced swim immobility at some doses and attenuated hippocampal and mitochondrial damage. Microbial community structure shifted toward controls, and medium-dose BHT altered faecal fatty acid profiles.

Conclusion BHT ameliorates depressive-like behaviour under chronic stress, with possible microbiota-gut-brain involvement, supporting further study as a dietary intervention.

Why it matters for you: Relevant to your interest in brain-body coupling, though the gut microbiota angle is outside your core imaging and EEG methods.
