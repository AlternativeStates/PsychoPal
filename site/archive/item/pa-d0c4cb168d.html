Long-term dysregulation of histone acetylation, BDNF expression, and the hippocampal transcriptome in adult rats exhibiting a depressive-like phenotype following repeated chlorpyrifos exposure

Ana C.R. Ribeiro, Elisa Hawkins, Fay M. Jahr, Mikhail Dozmorov, Joseph L. McClay, Laxmikant S. Deshpande
bioRxiv (Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory) · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.02.763669
Depression (incl. TRD/MDD)Pre-clinicalPreprintComputational / whole-brain modelling#bdnf#epigenetics#chlorpyrifos

Aim To investigate molecular mechanisms underlying a chronic depressive-like phenotype after repeated occupational-like chlorpyrifos exposure in rats.

Methods Adult rats given chlorpyrifos at 5 mg/kg/day for 21 days, assessed three months later; hippocampal Western blotting, ChIP for H3K9ac at the Bdnf promoter IV, and exploratory RNA-sequencing.

Results HDAC3 and HDAC5 were upregulated, and H3K9ac at the Bdnf promoter IV and BDNF protein were reduced. 100 genes were differentially expressed at FDR below 5%, including Igf2, Fgf14 and Slit2.

Conclusion The study indicates persistent epigenetic and transcriptional dysregulation linked to reduced hippocampal BDNF and altered plasticity pathways after exposure.

Why it matters for you: Concerns BDNF and epigenetic plasticity markers, which connect to your interest in neuroplastogen mechanisms, though this is preclinical and organophosphate-focused.
