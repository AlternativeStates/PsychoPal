Somnus, a tunable, open-source platform for automated EEG sleep scoring in physiological and disease mouse models.

Josh Brenner, Matthew S Caudill, Huda Y Zoghbi
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-10-12 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.05.756738
EEGEpilepsyHealthy volunteersPre-clinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingMachine learning / AIPre-clinical / invasive#sleep scoring#rodent eeg#open-source

Aim To automate rodent sleep staging from EEG and EMG across healthy and disease mouse models, with fast adaptation to fragmented sleep.

Methods Open-source Python package with a base model trained on recordings from six laboratories, a graphical interface directing users to low-confidence epochs, and fine-tuning from manual corrections.

Results Not reported in the abstract beyond the described workflow, which aims to yield accurate custom scorers after a few minutes of correction.

Conclusion Somnus lets researchers generate custom scorers for disease models without hand-scoring whole cohorts or building models from scratch.

Why it matters for you: Automated, adaptable sleep staging could support preclinical EEG work, though it is loosely connected to your human PET and psychedelic research.
