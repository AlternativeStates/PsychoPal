Mescaline enhances emotional empathy via 5-HT2A receptor activation.

Aaron Klaiber, Patrick Vizeli, Nimmy Varghese, Anne Eckert, Matthias E Liechti, Yasmin Schmid
Journal of psychopharmacology (Oxford, England) · 2026-11-19 · doi:10.1177/02698811261504423
PsilocybinLSDMescalineHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewed#mescaline#empathy#5-ht2a

Aim To assess the acute effects of several mescaline doses on empathy and plasma oxytocin, and the role of 5-HT2A receptors.

Methods Placebo-controlled, double-blind crossover study in 16 healthy participants receiving placebo, mescaline 100, 200, 400 and 800 mg, and 800 mg mescaline with the 5-HT2A antagonist ketanserin, using the Multifaceted Empathy Test and plasma oxytocin.

Results Mescaline dose-dependently enhanced emotional empathy and reduced cognitive empathy, and raised plasma oxytocin significantly only at 400 and 800 mg. Ketanserin blocked the empathy and oxytocin effects.

Conclusion Classic psychedelics acutely alter empathy and oxytocin through 5-HT2A receptor interaction, providing further evidence for this mechanism.

Why it matters for you: Links 5-HT2A receptor activation to social and emotional processing, relevant to your interest in brain-body and interoceptive effects of psychedelics.
