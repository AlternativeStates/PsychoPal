Endosomal mRNA transport coordinates local mitochondrial bioenergetics during polar fungal growth.

Johannes Postma, Pascal Künzel, Simon Wegmann, Kira Müntjes, Senthil-Kumar Devan, Srimeenakshi Sankaranarayanan, Stephan Krueger, Sebastian Hänsch, Philipp Westhoff, Stefanie Weidtkamp-Peters, Nick Wierckx, Lasse van Wijlick, Michael Feldbrügge
Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America · 2026-11-17 · doi:10.1073/pnas.2617588123
Peer reviewed

Background Mitochondrial function relies on the precise spatial coordination of protein synthesis and import.

Methods Here, we demonstrate that endosomal transport of mRNAs encoding mitochondrial proteins orchestrates local bioenergetics in infectious hyphae of Ustilago maydis.

Results Loss of Rrm4 leads to defects in mitochondrial import, resulting in altered physiology.

Conclusion We propose that endosome-coupled mRNA transport constitutes a fundamental layer of subcellular mitochondrial homeostasis, with implications extending from fungal pathogenicity to neuronal disease.
