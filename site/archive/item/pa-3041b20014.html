Intermittent theta burst stimulation to extend early ketamine response in treatment-resistant depression: A preliminary enriched randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled trial.

Tung-Ping Su, Mao-Hsuan Huang, Wan-Chen Chang, Wen-Han Chang, Pei-Chi Tu, Wei-Shan Huang, Wei-Chung Mao, Mu-Hong Chen
Psychiatry and clinical neurosciences · 2026-11-04 · doi:10.1111/pcn.70165
Ketamine / esketamineDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalPeer reviewedConnectivity / synchronyBrain stimulationClinical trial / evidence synthesis

Ketamine produces rapid antidepressant effects in treatment-resistant depression (TRD), but these benefits diminish within days. Whether intermittent theta-burst stimulation (iTBS) can sustain ketamine response and whether such effects are associated with changes in large-scale brain networks are unclear.
