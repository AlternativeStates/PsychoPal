The deep Earth origin of the Great Unconformity.

Santosh Dhungana, Nicolas Flament
Nature · 2026-11-04 · doi:10.1038/s41586-026-11156-5
Peer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modelling

Aim Here we show that continental-scale uplift and subsidence associated with long-wavelength dynamic topography12 due to mantle convection drove the formation and preservation of the Great Unconformity.

Methods Our models predict prolonged continental-scale Neoproterozoic uplift and long-term Phanerozoic subsidence for Laurentia, Baltica and North China.

Results The predicted prolonged continental uplift throughout Cryogenian times would have resulted in marked erosion due to the action of glaciers13. The large area of Laurentia and limited deformation at its margins explain why the Great Unconformity is best preserved and documented there.

Conclusion Our models predict cycles of dynamic topography that are broadly reflected in preserved sediment volume across continents1,14, and open new avenues for understanding the environments of Cambrian animal diversification.
