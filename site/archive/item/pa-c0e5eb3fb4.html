Longitudinal [¹⁸F]FDG PET changes map onto distinct cholinergic and serotonergic territories in autoimmune encephalitis.

Denise Cerne, Giulia Benvenuto, Stefano Raffa, Alessia Panza, Carolina Barone, Anastasia Lechiara, Emanuela Maria Mobilia, Giacomo Rebella, Pietro Mattioli, Beatrice Orso, Elisa Micalizzi, Dario Arnaldi, Flavio Villani, Luca Roccatagliata, Giampaola Pesce, Matteo Pardini, Silvia Morbelli, Antonio Uccelli, Luana Benedetti, Federico Massa
European journal of nuclear medicine and molecular imaging · 2026-11-23 · doi:10.1007/s00259-026-08246-x
ClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#fdg pet#juspace#autoimmune encephalitis

Aim Tests whether longitudinal [18F]FDG PET changes in autoimmune encephalitis align with specific neuromodulatory receptor territories.

Methods Retrospective secondary analysis of 22 patients with baseline and follow-up FDG PET, using JuSpace spatial correlation with 15 normative neurotransmitter maps and permutation-based inference with FDR correction.

Results Metabolic change correlated positively with VAChT (q = 0.023) and alpha4beta2 (q = 0.004) maps and negatively with 5-HT2A (q = 0.014). No clinical associations survived multiplicity correction.

Conclusion Metabolic change maps onto cholinergic and serotonergic territories, generating hypotheses about recovery, though spatial overlap does not imply directional signalling.

Why it matters for you: Applies receptor-space PET correlation methods akin to your serotonin PET and synaptic density work.
