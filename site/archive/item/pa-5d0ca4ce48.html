Levetiracetam, brivaracetam and piracetam at D₂ and 5-HT₂A receptors: a molecular docking and dynamics investigation.

Manu Kumar Shetty, Havagiray R Chitme, Devendra Mishra, Hemlata Nimesh
In silico pharmacology · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.1007/s40203-026-00789-z
PETPsychosis / schizophreniaPre-clinicalPeer reviewedMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)#molecular dynamics#5-ht2a#levetiracetam

Aim To test whether levetiracetam, brivaracetam and piracetam engage dopamine D2 and serotonin 5-HT2A receptors, possibly explaining neuropsychiatric side effects.

Methods Molecular docking into D2 and 5-HT2A orthosteric pockets, followed by three independent 50 ns explicit-membrane molecular dynamics replicates per system (18 trajectories in total).

Results Ligands stayed bound in all frames, but per-residue contacts varied widely between replicates. Occupancy of the pocket was sustained by hydrophobic contact, notably Leu229 at 5-HT2A, in 49-74% of frames.

Conclusion Whether these interactions are pharmacologically meaningful requires radioligand binding measurement rather than further simulation.

Why it matters for you: Computational pharmacology of receptor binding parallels your molecular dynamics work on tryptamines, though it is outside your core psychedelic focus.
