MLE EEG Toolbox: A Personal AI Laboratory Assistant for Literature-Informed and Reproducible M/EEG Research

X. Liu, W. Chen, B. Wan, S. Wang, Z. Lin, X. Zhang, L. Dong, S. Hu, X. Guo, R. Cui, R. Ge
biorxiv (neuroscience) · 2026-11-20 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.18.767737
EEGMEGClinicalPreprintSpectral / aperiodic / waveformConnectivity / synchronyPreprocessing / source / toolsMachine learning / AI#eeg#meg#reproducibility

Aim To build a locally run AI assistant that links methodological literature, natural-language instructions and reproducible EEG and MEG analysis.

Methods Software framework combining a constrained local language model, literature retrieval, provenance tracking and cross-platform tests, applied to public EEG and MEG datasets.

Results Action-selection accuracy was 85.6% for EEG and 95.5% for MEG. 142 cross-platform tests showed close numerical agreement. Spectral, network, decoding and source analyses were demonstrated on public data.

Conclusion The toolbox offers a researcher-controlled, reproducible environment running on standard personal computers without external AI APIs.

Why it matters for you: Relevant to your EEG and MEG analysis workflows and reproducibility, and could reduce friction in your neurodynamics pipelines.
