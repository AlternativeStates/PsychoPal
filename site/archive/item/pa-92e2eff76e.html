Ketamine versus Diazepam for Pediatric MRI Sedation in Northern Malawi: A Retrospective Analysis.

Lin Liu, Joel Moyo, Blessed Kondowe, Patrick Manda, Jingping Zhang, Junyi Ren
Malawi medical journal : the journal of Medical Association of Malawi · 2026-10-23 · doi:10.4314/mmj.v38i3.8
Ketamine / esketamineClinicalPeer reviewed#ketamine#paediatric#sedation

Aim To compare ketamine and diazepam for paediatric MRI sedation in a low-resource hospital in northern Malawi.

Methods Single-centre retrospective study of children aged 0-14 years, ketamine (n = 20) versus diazepam (n = 36), August 2025 to March 2026, with Ramsay Sedation Score success and MRI completion outcomes.

Results Ketamine gave faster onset (1.55 vs 3.43 min, P < 0.01) and higher sedation success (95% vs 67%, P < 0.05), with longer recovery (58.6 vs 43.1 min). Adverse event rates were similar.

Conclusion Ketamine provided faster, more reliable paediatric MRI sedation with a favourable safety profile, provided monitoring and anaesthetic oversight are in place.

Why it matters for you: Only loosely related to your work; it is a clinical sedation comparison rather than a psychedelic or neuroplasticity study.
