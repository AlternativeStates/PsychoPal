Preclinical Evaluation of [ 18 F]Itareparib, a brain penetrant PARP1-selective radioligand, in Glioma Rat Model and Nonhuman Primate.

Peng Wen Tan, Nira Hernandez-Martin, Claudia Esposito-Zapero, Christina Sun, Takuya Toyonaga, Ming-Ǫiang Zheng, Michael Kapinos, Monique A Thomas, Syed Faraz Nejati, Clement Korsah, Daniel Holden, Baosheng Chen, Jorge Sepulcre, Richard E Carson, Yiyun Huang, Henk M De Feyter, Zhengxin Cai
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-10-19 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.16.758050
PETPre-clinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)

Aim In this study, we evaluated if [ 18 F]Itareparib, a brain-penetrant, non-trapping, PARP1-selective radioligand with reversible kinetics, could improve brain tumor diagnosis and enable target occupancy studies in neurooncology.

Methods [ 18 F]Itareparib was synthesized and evaluated in an orthotopic rat model of BT142 IDH1-mutant gliomas. Dynamic PET scans were performed with or without the administration of the PARP1-selective inhibitor AZD9574.

Results AZD9574 reduced tumor uptake by 77%, supporting the PARP1-specific binding of [ 18 F]Itareparib in the tumors. In the NHP, [ 18 F]Itareparib showed rapid brain uptake, and V T decreased by 21 ± 6 % after blocking with AZD9574.

Conclusion These findings support further development of [ 18 F]Itareparib for the quantitative PET imaging of PARP1 in brain tumors.
