EXPRESS: Blood-free full quantification of dynamic TSPO PET imaging with [11C]PBR28.

Ludovica Brusaferri, Alexander C Whitehead, Leonardo Barzon, Lucia Maccioni, Zeynab Alshelh, Minhae Kim, Antonio Micoli, Nicola Toschi, Matteo Ferrante, Marianna Inglese, Ciprian Catana, Jodi Gilman, Mattia Veronese, Oliver D Howes, Tiago Marques, Maria Antonietta Nettis, Valeria Mondelli, Carmine M Pariante, Federico E Turkheimer, Enrico Grisan, Marco Loggia
Journal of cerebral blood flow and metabolism : official journal of the International Society of Cerebral Blood Flow and Metabolism · 2026-11-12 · doi:10.1177/0271678x261508659
Peer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingMultimodal imaging (PET/DTI)

Background Quantitative dynamic PET imaging requires metabolite-corrected arterial input functions, traditionally obtained through invasive arterial blood sampling, limiting broader adoption of quantitative kinetic modelling.

Methods We developed ASTRA, a deep-learning-based framework for arterial input function estimation from dynamic brain TSPO PET.

Results ASTRA-derived quantitative estimates agreed with arterial measurements (VT: CCC=0.658-0.805), with higher uncertainty associated with greater error. In the clinical cohort, ASTRA reproduced SUVR-based group differences using DVR (p<0.001), except in the pituitary gland (p=0.12).

Conclusion These findings demonstrate the feasibility of multi-site, non-invasive quantitative TSPO PET.
