Pathway-selective Gi1, but not Gq, protein activation distinguishes hallucinogenic from non-hallucinogenic serotonin 5-HT2A receptor agonists in the human cortex.

Patricia Miranda-Azpiazu, Rebeca Diez-Alarcia, Itziar Muneta-Arrate, Aintzane García-Bea, Rafael Maldonado, Benito Morentin, Javier González-Maeso, J Javier Meana
Neurochemistry international · 2026-11-06 · doi:10.1016/j.neuint.2026.106285
Non-hallucinogenic analoguesPeer reviewed

Aim Here, the differential signaling patterns between hallucinogenic and non-hallucinogenic 5-HT 2A R agonists were evaluated in post-mortem human prefrontal cortex using [ 35 S]GTPγS binding assays coupled with antibody immunoprecipitation against different …

Results The hallucinogenic drugs (±)DOI, TCB-2, and LSD activated both G q/11 and G i1 proteins through 5-HT 2A Rs. The alkylating agent NEM attenuated agonist-mediated G i1 protein activation with null effect on transduction through G q/11 proteins, supporting the involvement of inhibitory G proteins in the activity of hallucinogenic 5-HT 2A R drugs.

Conclusion These findings may help explain the differential behavioral responses elicited by psychedelic and non-psychedelic 5-HT 2A R agonists.
