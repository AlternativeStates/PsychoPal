Anterior cingulate cortex neurons parse speakers in a natural cocktail party.

Arthur Lefevre, Vikram Pal Singh, Timothy J Tyree, Jingwen Li, Pia Giraudet, Jean-René Duhamel, Cory T Miller
Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America · 2026-11-24 · doi:10.1073/pnas.2601809123
Peer reviewed#anterior cingulate#marmoset#auditory scene

Aim How anterior cingulate cortex neurons parse social partners' vocalisations in a noisy, natural colony environment.

Methods Single-neuron recordings from ACC (area 24) in freely moving marmosets engaged in spontaneous vocal exchanges within a colony setting.

Results ACC neurons selectively encoded calls from a social partner versus other callers, preserved during temporal overlap. Population activity also represented vocal production, call type and turn-taking context.

Conclusion ACC neurons appear to parse conspecific speakers during natural communication, linking auditory scene analysis with social monitoring.

Why it matters for you: Freely moving single-unit recordings in a natural social context sit outside your human imaging work but connect to your interest in brain–body and social neuroscience.
