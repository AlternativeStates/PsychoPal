Multi-receptor whole-brain modeling reveals affinity-driven chemoarchitectural mechanism of virtual atomoxetine action

Martí Monge-Asensio, Murat Demirtaş, Thomas A Pfeffer, Gorka Zamora‐López, Jakub Vohryzek, Yonatan Sanz-Perl, Ruud L. van den Brink, Tobias H. Donner, Morten L. Kringelbach, Gustavo Deco
Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience · 2026-11-04 · doi:10.3389/fncom.2026.1854866
Peer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modelling

Linking molecular mechanisms with large-scale brain dynamics remains a fundamental challenge in computational psychiatry. Receptor-informed whole-brain models have provided mechanistic insights into pharmacological interventions, yet they have largely relied on single-receptor topographies that cannot explain the collective response of multiple receptor systems.
