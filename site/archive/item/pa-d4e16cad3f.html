The first 1000 days: Modeling language learning from individual children's everyday speech.

Hadas Raviv, Arkadii Tsyhanov, Kira Gousios, Aja Altenhof, Haocheng Wang, Berlin Chen, Ofri Raviv, Tal Rosenwein, Casey Lew-Williams, Liat Hasenfratz, Uri Hasson
Science (New York, N.Y.) · 2026-11-05 · doi:10.1126/science.aeh9311
ClinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingMachine learning / AI

Background Children learn to understand and speak from the speech around them, yet how that everyday input shapes learning remains unresolved.

Methods We trained cognitively feasible, child-specific models on longitudinal home recordings from eight children across their first 1000 days of life.

Conclusion These findings show that everyday experience contains sufficient structure for language learning and establish child-specific modeling for testing developmental theories.
