How Does Psilocybin Affect Daily Life Experience and Behavior? Protocol For a Multimethod Ambulatory Assessment Study Within a Clinical Trial of Psilocybin for Depression.

Andreea Sutu, Deanna Kaplan, Charles L Raison, Christina J Sauder, Santiago Jose Arconada Alvarez, Nicole S Pozzo, Zainab Salako, Zhuo Job Chen, Roman Palistky
medRxiv : the preprint server for health sciences · 2026-11-12 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.10.26367628
PsilocybinDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalPeer reviewedClinical trial / evidence synthesis#psilocybin#depression#ecological momentary assessment

Aim Protocol for characterising daily-life processes that predict differential responses to psilocybin in adults with depression.

Methods Prospective observational multimethod study embedded in a trial of 25 mg psilocybin in up to 141 adults with major depressive disorder, using ecological momentary assessment, voice diaries and passive audio sampling.

Conclusion Findings will show whether daily affect, social and reflective factors predict outcomes after psilocybin; this is a study protocol without results.

Why it matters for you: Ambulatory and daily-life measurement of psilocybin response complements your clinical psychedelic pharmacology work in depression.
