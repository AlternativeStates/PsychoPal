Rotigotine Behenate ameliorates depression in Parkinson's disease through NF-κB-mediated anti-inflammation and BDNF-mediated neuroprotection.

Mengting Wu, Zhicong Yin, Yumeng Chen, Caiyi Qin, Mengting Zheng, Ronghao Zhang, Wenjing Lv, Yuanyingzhu Liang, Jingwei Tian, Xin Yu
International immunopharmacology · 2026-11-15 · doi:10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117696
SSRIs / antidepressantsDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)Parkinson'sPre-clinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingPre-clinical / invasive#parkinson's disease#bdnf#depression

Aim To test whether rotigotine behenate ameliorates depression in Parkinson's disease and to identify the anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective mechanisms involved.

Methods A combined MPTP and chronic unpredictable mild stress model in male C57BL/6 mice, compared with fluoxetine, with behavioural assays, neurotransmitter measures in striatum and prefrontal cortex, and NF-κB, BDNF/TrkB and dendritic spine analyses; QNZ was used to inhibit NF-κB.

Results Rotigotine behenate improved motor and depressive-like behaviour and restored regional dopamine and serotonin balance. It suppressed NF-κB/NLRP3 neuroinflammation and activated BDNF/TrkB/PI3K/Akt/GSK-3β signalling, reversing spine loss. NF-κB inhibition only partly reproduced these effects.

Conclusion Rotigotine behenate alleviates depression in Parkinson's disease preclinically through anti-inflammatory and BDNF-mediated neuroprotection, with some NF-κB-independent mechanisms.

Why it matters for you: BDNF/TrkB signalling and synaptic plasticity in a depression model overlap with your interest in neuroplastogenic mechanisms, though it is preclinical and dopaminergic.
