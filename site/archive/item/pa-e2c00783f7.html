Personalized Prediction of Differences in fMRI Connectivity Estimates Across Preprocessing Pipelines

Y. Maximov, A. Volkov, T. Chattopadhyay, P. M. Thompson, I. Gabitov
biorxiv (neuroscience) · 2026-11-06 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.04.763874
fMRIDementia / Alzheimer'sClinicalPreprintConnectivity / synchronyComputational / whole-brain modellingPreprocessing / source / tools

Background Longitudinal functional MRI of the human brain is valuable for tracking changes in functional connectivity (FC) associated with disease progression or treatment response.

Methods Even so, measured FC values depend on preprocessing, and nuisance regression and temporal filtering affect individuals differently. We examined whether we could use earlier scans to predict how preprocessing would affect a later scan.

Results This advantage persisted after accounting for acquisition variables. The ADNI-trained model reduced mean error in ten Midnight Scan Club participants without refitting.

Conclusion These results support using personal preprocessing history to predict changes in connectivity estimates on newly acquired scans.
