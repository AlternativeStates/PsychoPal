A multi-institutional investigation of psilocybin's effects on mouse behavior.

Odilia D Lu, Katrina White, Kendall B Raymond, Christine Liu, Alexandra S Klein, Nathaniel Green, Sam Vaillancourt, Austin J Gallagher, Lena Shindy, Alyssa Li, Kira E Wallquist, Ruoxian Li, Mila Zou, Austen B Casey, Natalie Perez, Muhammad Asim, Setareh Metanat, Kelsey R Logas, Vivan Chhaya, Alexander S Enos, Ellie M Ho, Lindsay P Cameron, Matthew B Pomrenze, Jason M Tucciarone, Vikaas S Sohal, Mazen A Kheirbek, Andrea M Gomez, Stephan Lammel, Boris D Heifets, Robert C Malenka
Nature neuroscience · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.1038/s41593-026-02500-z
PsilocybinPre-clinicalPeer reviewed

Studies reporting novel therapeutic effects of psychedelic drugs are rapidly emerging. However, the reproducibility and reliability of these findings could remain uncertain for years. Here, we implemented a multi-institutional collaborative approach to define the robust and replicable effects of the psychedelic drug psilocybin on mouse behavior.
