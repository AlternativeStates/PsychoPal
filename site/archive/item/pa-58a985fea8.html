Fear learning-induced brain dynamics predict individual extinction memory expression following transcranial magnetic stimulation.

Kai Zhang, Lihan Cui, B Isabel Moallem, Hani Meelad, Zakiya Atiyah, Muhammad Badarnee, Isabella Mason, Zhenfu Wen, Mark S George, Mohammed R Milad
Nature communications · 2026-10-05 · doi:10.1038/s41467-026-78345-8
TMS / TMS-EEGHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewedComputational / whole-brain modellingBrain stimulation

Background Fear learning and extinction unfold as time-dependent processes.

Methods Herein, we examined whether fear learning dynamically reorganizes brain activity and whether such reorganization predicts subsequent extinction-memory expression under transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS)-modulated extinction.

Results Using coactivation pattern analysis with a hidden Markov model, we identified a fear-learning-induced brain state characterized by increased engagement and transition uncertainty after conditioning.

Conclusion These findings demonstrate that fear learning reshapes brain-state dynamics and identifies a candidate biomarker of neuromodulation-linked extinction-memory expression.
