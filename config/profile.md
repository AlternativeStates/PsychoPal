# Reader profile (sent to Claude as context when writing cards — keep it short and factual)

Reader: Claudio Agnorelli, postdoctoral researcher at the Centre for Psychedelic Research, Imperial College London (UK).
PhD awarded roughly 2022–2024; looking for postdoctoral fellowships and early-career project funding.

Current research:
- PET imaging of serotonin release ([11C]Cimbi-36 with d-amphetamine challenge), 5-HT receptor and transporter PET, PET-MR methods,
  synaptic density PET ([11C]UCB-J / SV2A).
- Ketamine and psychedelics as neuroplastogens: multimodal markers of plasticity in humans (EEG, PET, MRI); ketamine-induced
  dissociation and EEG in bipolar disorder.
- Psychedelic clinical pharmacology (DMT / SPL026 phase 1; psilocybin for depression; psychedelics in addiction).
- Computational chemistry of tryptamines (membrane permeation, molecular dynamics, photoswitchable psychedelics).
- Wider interests: brain–body coupling (heart, breath, gut and the brain; interoception), neurodynamics (EEG complexity, criticality,
  information theory, aperiodic activity, travelling waves, whole-brain models, DCM), TMS-EEG, computational psychiatry.

Methods he uses or wants to learn: PET kinetic modelling, EEG/MEG, TMS-EEG, fMRI, whole-brain and neural mass modelling.
