The influence of the 1/f component in non-invasive transcranial electrical stimulation waveforms on neural oscillations

Hisato Nakazono, Keisuke Kimura, Akinori Takeda, Emi Yamada, Tsubasa Mitsutake, Takanori Taniguchi, Katsuya Ogata
Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation · 2026-11-18 · doi:10.1186/s12984-026-02228-8
EEGHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewedSpectral / aperiodic / waveformComputational / whole-brain modellingBrain stimulation#1/f component#tes#alpha oscillations

Aim To determine whether including the 1/f component in transcranial electrical stimulation waveforms changes neural oscillations compared with stimulation without it.

Methods Randomised, sham-controlled crossover study in 20 analysed healthy young adults receiving 20 minutes of individual spectrum-characteristic stimulation, aperiodic-removed stimulation or sham, with resting EEG before and after and generalised linear mixed models.

Results Aperiodic-removed stimulation significantly increased alpha power compared with sham, whereas individual spectrum stimulation did not differ from sham. Neither condition altered the aperiodic exponent or offset.

Conclusion Contrary to expectations, the spectral composition of personalised waveforms, including the 1/f component, may be a relevant parameter warranting further study.
