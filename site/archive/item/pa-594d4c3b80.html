Cortical-fluidic dimensional mapping isolates GRIN2A-driven dysconnectivity and pharmacological network degradation in schizophrenia.

Wanus Srimaharaj
Journal of psychiatric research · 2026-11-04 · doi:10.1016/j.jpsychires.2026.10.026
EEGPsychosis / schizophreniaHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewedConnectivity / synchronyComputational / whole-brain modellingPreprocessing / source / tools

Background Standard connectivity models fail to capture the transient spatial kinematics underlying schizophrenia pathophysiology.

Methods The algorithm processed over 7000 memory testing sessions from 364 healthy participants. The validation phase evaluated a pre-existing clinical repository comprising 300 de-identified psychiatric records.

Results Cuneus dysconnectivity links strictly to global symptom severity. The methodology establishes an objective biomarker.

Conclusion The system links molecular glutamatergic vulnerabilities to macroscopic neural degradation in treatment-resistant psychiatric conditions.
